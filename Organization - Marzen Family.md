# The Marzen Family

> **Type:** Infrastructure family
> **Location:** Penthouse Block (Ridge)
> **Function:** Electrical distribution monopoly
> **Key asset:** 3 MW thorium micro-reactor

The Marzen Family controls electrical distribution through ownership of a 3 MW thorium micro-reactor—the only local power source capable of reliable output.

## The Token System

Electricity is distributed via a ceramic token system:
1. Residents purchase tokens representing kilowatt-hours
2. Tokens are inserted into household meters
3. Power flows accordingly

**Benefits:**
- Predictable revenue
- Prevents theft
- Power can be cut for non-payment without affecting other customers

## Key Members

**Telsin Marzen** — Father. Eyesight failing. Original technical knowledge holder.

**Dara Marzen** — Daughter. Has taken over certification accounts as father declines. Demonstrates the family's careful succession planning.

Her arranged marriage to Qirren Val (both are gay, both need heirs) will unite the two most essential infrastructure monopolies.

## The Reactor

**Status:** Old but stable

**Maintenance:** Constant monitoring and inherited technical knowledge

**Critical fact:** Replacement is impossible. The knowledge to build a new reactor doesn't exist in ya-Sattra. The Marzens maintain what previous generations built because the alternative is darkness.

This makes their technical expertise irreplaceable—and their family succession planning essential.

## Interdependence

The Marzens cannot operate alone:
- **Need Val water** for reactor cooling
- **Need Galen intelligence** to anticipate district changes
- **Provide electricity** that Vals need for water pumps
- **Provide electricity** that Galens need for printing operations

None of the three families could destroy the others without crippling their own operations.

## Position in District

Like the Vals and Galens, the Marzens see themselves as:
- Respectable mercantile operators, not criminals
- Maintaining position through technical competence rather than violence
- Obligated to provide essential services reliably
- Equals to Medina Quarter merchants despite Rotten Quarter location

The token system exemplifies their approach—orderly, predictable, professional. Not the chaos of the Middens or the violence of the Iron Yards, but systematic infrastructure serving paying customers.
