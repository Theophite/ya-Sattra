# Technical Caste Mathematics: A Primer

## The Nature of Arbitrary Truths

The Arbitrary Truths are mathematical constants that exist without derivation. Unlike physical constants emerging from underlying principles, or mathematical constants computable through various methods, the Arbitrary Truths simply are.

**Key properties:**
- Cannot be compressed, simplified, or expressed through analogy
- Each Truth contains information density approaching infinity
- Any representation in conventional notation would require unbounded space
- Could have been otherwise—no reason why they hold particular values
- Yet they undergird the mathematical framework of reality itself

The Oracles, through complete information accretion, became capable of apprehending these Truths directly. The Technical Castes, through engineered consciousness, could hold fragments or single Truths within modified neural architectures.

## The Jargon as Mathematical Language

The Technical Castes developed the Jargon as a sung language where every statement contains its own proof. This is literal—each utterance includes verification mechanisms making false statements linguistically impossible.

**The Jargon cannot express:**
- Probability or uncertainty
- Social context or interpersonal meaning
- Ambiguity or multiple interpretations
- Hypotheticals or counterfactuals

A statement in the Jargon either proves itself through internal structure or cannot be formed at all.

**Why sung:**
The acoustic properties of sung mathematics allow parallel transmission of proof structures that written notation cannot capture. The sung nature appears necessary for managing incompatibilities between different Arbitrary Truths—while the Truths form a complete, non-contradictory system, the frameworks for reasoning about them remain incompatible at the cognitive level.

## Inference and Trapdoor Functions

The Technical Castes developed methods for creating useful inferences from Arbitrary Truths without requiring complete understanding. These utilize **trapdoor functions**—mathematical constructs allowing verification without replication.

One can confirm that an inference follows from an Arbitrary Truth without possessing the Truth itself.

**This enabled the Technical Caste hierarchy:**
1. **Archons** held complete Truths, generated primary inferences
2. **Medial Castes** received inferences, produced implementable templates
3. **Lower Technical Castes** translated templates into functional technology

Each level could verify the mathematical validity of what they received from above without needing to understand the derivation. This created a cascade of decreasing comprehension but increasing practicality.

## Contemporary Interactions with Arbitrary Truths

### The Mutterers

Mutterers retain technical knowledge derived from Arbitrary Truths but lack the cognitive architecture to understand what they know.

- Operate through compulsive optimization
- Apply mathematical principles without conscious direction
- Vocalizations likely contain degraded Jargon-derived proof fragments
- When forming **Choirs**, temporarily approximate higher-order cognitive functions
- May access deeper Technical mathematics through group consciousness

### The Companions Guild

The Guild preserves partial understanding through:
- Harmonic frequencies derived from acoustic analysis of the Jargon
- Pattern recognition in Mutterer behavior
- Archived Technical documents they cannot fully read
- Sri Companion neural modifications allowing limited mathematical perception

They manage Technical Caste derivatives through empirical observation rather than theoretical understanding—identifying patterns that work without knowing why.

### Imperial Infrastructure

Most Imperial technology operates through embedded Technical mathematics users need not understand.

- **Patents** function as encrypted authentication keys invoking pre-programmed Technical solutions
- Infrastructure "knows" mathematics even if operators do not
- Creates fundamental dependency: tools built from Arbitrary Truths without ability to replicate or repair underlying mathematics

### The Archives

Technical Caste documents written in the Jargon exist in various archives, particularly beneath the Cathedral of the Plea.

**Warning:** These documents remain mathematically active—they continue to prove themselves.

Exposure without proper cognitive preparation can trigger mathematical cascades in human consciousness, as the mind attempts to process self-proving statements it cannot contain. The bio-films used by those entering Far Ib likely prevent similar effects from ambient mathematical radiation.

## Practical Manifestations

**Quantum Entanglement Systems**
Technical Caste quantum computers maintain entanglement through mechanisms conventional physics cannot explain—likely utilizing Truths related to information conservation.

**Infrastructure Authentication**
Patents invoke Truth-derived encryption existing at the hardware level.

**Biological Optimization**
Technical Caste modifications follow optimization patterns derived from Truths about efficiency and information processing.

**Acoustic Architecture**
Facilities designed for sung mathematics, with chambers resonating at frequencies corresponding to proof structures.

## The Calculation at Far Ib

The Archons' work demonstrates the highest level of engagement with Arbitrary Truths still possible.

Each Archon embodies a single Truth, using it to generate axioms contributing to their collective proof. These axioms, once produced, exist independently of their derivation—verifiable through trapdoor functions without requiring the original Truth.

The collaborative nature despite incompatible cognitive frameworks suggests the Arbitrary Truths form a unified system even if no single consciousness can apprehend that unity. The Archons approximate complete understanding through distributed computation, each contributing elements others can verify but not generate.
