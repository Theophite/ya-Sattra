# The Labor Castes

> **Status:** Specialized industrial workers
> **Common feature:** Bodies engineered for hazardous tasks
> **Common cost:** Each caste has specific failure mode

Labor castes were engineered for work that would kill or cripple baseline humans. Each variant trades long-term health for short-term capability.

## Ironbone (Labor Variant)

**Type specimen:** Deval Sammat, 47, dock supervisor

**Physical characteristics:**
- Nearly eight feet tall, frame heavy with bone mass
- Hands enormous, palms wide enough to wrap cargo strapping without mechanical aid
- Calcium deposition continues throughout life

**Failure mode:** Joints stiffen progressively. At 47, Deval no longer lifts cargo—knees limited, hips grinding. Perhaps ten more years of mobility before chair-bound like his father.

**Knowledge persists:** His experience with cargo handling remains valuable. He knows which loads require which crew compositions, how to sequence operations for maximum efficiency.

## Redback

**Type specimen:** Kora Brennam, 33, cargo handler

**Physical characteristics:**
- Around two meters tall
- Enhanced spinal articulation and musculature
- Characteristic ridge of muscle along spine (visible through clothing)
- Can carry three times body weight for short distances

**Failure mode:** Binary. No decline, no gradual deterioration. One day you work a full shift; that night or the next morning, you stop. Her mother died at 41 mid-shift carrying machine parts. Her aunt made it to 93.

**Philosophy:** This is the trade her caste made. No decline, no dependence, no years of watching capability fade.

## Springheel

**Type specimen:** Venn Torith, 25, high-rigging worker

**Physical characteristics:**
- Five foot seven (short by Imperial standards, average for caste)
- Leg proportions slightly wrong—calves larger than expected
- Tendons visible beneath skin
- Standing leap of twelve feet vertical, higher with running start

**Failure mode:** Tendons tear without warning. His uncle lost use of right leg at 30. His cousin died at 22 when failure during high work sent him falling.

**Community:** Springheels watch each other during high work. Have developed techniques for predicting tendon stress, methods for managing risk.

## Beamcrawler

**Type specimen:** Ossin Thul, 24, infrastructure inspector

**Physical characteristics:**
- Six feet tall, but proportions wrong
- Arms reaching past knees
- Torso short, limbs long
- Gait awkward on flat ground, fluid in scaffolding

**Function:** Inspects spaces too cramped for normal access—frameworks beneath elevated roads, interior structures of industrial towers.

**Relationship:** Lives with Tenna, a Ductworker (four foot six). Their apartment is a compromise—fixtures at heights awkward for both. They want children but don't know if the cross works.

## Ductworker

**Type specimen:** Minni Cavel, 29, ventilation maintenance

**Physical characteristics:**
- Four foot eight, slight build
- Ribcage more cartilage than bone—can compress to half diameter
- Joints dislocate voluntarily
- Neural architecture eliminates claustrophobia entirely

**Function:** Crawls through passages built for pipes and cables—shafts that collapse her chest, three-dimensional mazes that would panic baseline humans.

**Community:** Ductworkers gather weekly in basement spaces sized for their proportions, sharing meals and information. Conversations would sound strange to outsiders—detailed descriptions of shaft configurations, debates about compression techniques.

## Orevet (Furnace-Seer)

**Type specimen:** Keth Vorran, 47, failing

**Physical characteristics:**
- Visual spectrum extended into infrared
- Irises nearly black with hints of deep red
- Pupils contract to pinpoints in moderate light

**Function:** Sees temperature gradients and thermal anomalies—reads "shimmer" the way other castes read text.

**Failure mode:** Eyes begin failing in 40s. No treatment exists. Keth hasn't reported the degradation—would trigger reassignment. She is storing what she sees, building a memory archive that she hopes will persist when direct perception stops.

## Ashrat (Cinder-Blooded)

**Type specimen:** Senna Korrad, 34, heat-exchanger technician and cook

**Physical characteristics:**
- Grey undertone from dermal calcium compounds (deepens with age)
- Sweat contains silicates leaving permanent dusty residue
- Runs hot, requires enormous caloric intake

**Metabolism:** During shifts, eats constantly—body would cannibalize itself within hours otherwise. Off-shift, Ashrat are notorious gourmands—heightened sensitivity to flavor compounds.

**Culture:** Ashrat cuisine emphasizes complexity within richness. Senna is writing a cookbook—attempting to compile oral tradition into written form.

## Karst (Deep-Dweller)

**Type specimen:** Oma Tessik, 41, pressure-seal technician

**Physical characteristics:**
- Under five feet, barrel chest (expanded lung capacity for thin atmospheres)
- Eyes enormous, perhaps twice baseline size (adapted for bioluminescence)
- Perpetual crouch, spine curved differently
- Movement awkward at surface, fluid underground

**Function:** Works the Depths—tunnel systems where pressure differentials and minimal light would kill surface-adapted humans.

**Correspondence:** Receiving letters from Voss Drell (Karst in ya-Sattra arcology). They have been writing for fourteen months. He has proposed marriage. She has not yet answered—the obstacles are substantial.
