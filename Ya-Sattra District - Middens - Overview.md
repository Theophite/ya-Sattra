# The Middens: District Overview

> **Population:** ~15,000 (estimates vary: Bureau says 12,000; Hanged Men records suggest 18,000)
> **Geography:** Promontory of flooded pre-Interdict foundations jutting into the Rift
> **Core resource:** Water - controls everything
> **Power structure:** Hanged Men (Gallows) vs sewer-fisher clans (Hollow-Egress)

Ya-Sattra's poorest district. Seven tenements on partially flooded foundations, where water surrounds and permeates everything.

## Geographic Overview

The Middens occupy a promontory jutting into the Rift—ya-Sattra's harbor carved from the former undersea canyon of Monterey Bay. The terrain follows a gradient from water's edge inward:

- **North/West:** Open water, fishing boats, salvage vessels at improvised docks
- **South:** Rises toward the Ridge (fallen arcology providing Rotten Quarter's northern border)
- **East:** Blurs into Iron Yards without clear demarcation

## The Seven Tenements

| Building | Pop. | Function | Controller |
|----------|------|----------|------------|
| **Gallows** | ~200 | Hanged Men HQ, water control | Hanged Men (Knot) |
| **Bright-Spire** | ~2,700 | Residential under occupation | Chain-Men |
| **Hollow-Egress** | ~1,800 | Salvage access (Lift-3) | Five-clan cooperative |
| **Sump-Gate** | ~1,500 | Pumping station, Valve-Saint Parish | Mixed |
| **Lantern-Heaps** | ~2,200 | Electronics salvage, electrical monopoly | Cho clan |
| **Knife-Edge** | ~1,600 | Metalworking (15° lean) | Independent forges |
| **Vetch-Rise** | ~2,500 | Transient housing, sole land entry | Yen Tam Society (weak) |

## How Buildings Connect

**Only Vetch-Rise connects directly to mainland.** Everyone entering or leaving on foot passes through Vetch-Rise or pays for boat transport. This explains Yen Tam Society influence despite Hanged Men dominance elsewhere.

**The Gallows stands centrally** - height allows surveillance, Lockworks determines water distribution.

**Bright-Spire rises from the harbor itself** - foundations submerged, accessible by boat or bridges to Hollow-Egress. Defensible but isolated from commerce.

**Hollow-Egress sits between Bright-Spire and Gallows** - Lift-3 makes it the economic heart of salvage despite Gallows' political centrality.

**The Cho gondola** (Lantern-Heaps to Ridge) provides the only direct Middens-Ridge connection.

## District Relationships

The tenements relate through functional interdependence:

```
Gallows (water) → Hollow-Egress (deep infrastructure)
                → Hollow-Egress (salvage) → Knife-Edge (processing)
                                          → Knife-Edge (cables) → Lantern-Heaps (electrical)
                                                                → Lantern-Heaps (power) → Sump-Gate (pumps)
                                                                                        → Sump-Gate → Bright-Spire (aquaculture)
                                                                                                    → Bright-Spire (crane) → Vetch-Rise (cargo)
```

No single tenement can be destroyed without cascading effects. The Hanged Men exploit this stability while being constrained by it.

**With the Iron Yards (east):** Symbiotic. Yards provide salvage; Middens provide labor and housing.

**With the Ridge (south):** Aspirational. Cho gondola provides physical connection; social distance remains absolute.

**With the Rift (north/west):** Maritime. Fishing and salvage provide food and opportunity.

## The Central Concern: Water

The Hanged Men's power derives from controlling distribution through the Lockworks facility. The sewer-fishers' survival depends on access to flooded infrastructure. The Valve-Saint Parish worships in an overflow chamber where seawater seeps through the floor.

**Understanding who controls water, who needs it, and what they trade for it explains most of what happens in the Middens.**

## Major Organizations

**Hanged Men:** Criminal organization controlling water infrastructure. Seven-member Knot leads from the Gallows. Three crews: Valvehands (water), Iron Yards Boys (salvage extraction), Chain-Men (enforcement).
→ See: Organization - Hanged Men.md

**Sewer-Fisher Clans:** Six families controlling different aspects of the hidden economy. Essential for infrastructure maintenance, legally invisible.
→ See: Caste - Sewer-Fishers - Overview.md and individual clan files

**Valve-Saint Parish:** Penitent congregation in Sump-Gate. Curate Aram es-Thabit conducts services incorporating industrial reality. Operates grain dole.

**Chain-Men:** Ironbone Warborn enforcers occupying Bright-Spire. Terminal variant approaching 3 meters. Led by Garren Fen XI.

## Climate and Atmosphere

- **Weather:** Perpetually damp. Fog most mornings, rain frequent.
- **Air:** Salt from harbor, coal smoke from Knife-Edge forges, industrial runoff smell
- **Sound:** Water (always), hammering, transformer hum, Mutterer vocalizations
- **Colors:** Rust, grey, tarnished copper; Hanged Men rust-red and teal; Cho rainbow lights
