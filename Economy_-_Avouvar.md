## Core Principle: House Allocation

The Avouvar economy operates through distribution rather than markets. You belong to your house; your house allocates based on your position. There is no labor market—house membership is biological destiny determined at birth.

The autofactory eliminates production economics. The Smelters don't produce metal through labor and capital; they collect outputs from terminals that extrude according to schedules no one controls. Production is exogenous; only distribution and exchange remain.

Immortality transforms economic thinking completely. What does saving mean when you might live seven hundred years? Elder Koratvelan has managed his faction for centuries; his planning operates on scales that make any human institution seem ephemeral.

**Historical parallel:** Greek oikos economy combined with Bronze Age redistributive palace systems. The autofactory functions like a palace's agricultural hinterland; the houses distribute what it provides.

**External trade:** Factors at San Juan negotiate in obols, calculate margins, seek profit. The factorias are where Asovoë's distributive economy interfaces with the exchange economy of the broader world.

## Major Institutions

### House Vroëszka (Smelter Precinct)

**What it does:** Controls the primary autofactory terminals producing bulk metals that make Asovoë worth trading with. Approximately 8,000 members governed by seven elders over 600 years old, nominally headed by 870-year-old Matriarch Czenovaë (who abandoned leadership four centuries ago).

**Cash flow in:**
- Metal trade: Three major terminals producing 50,000-100,000 obols equivalent of metal monthly when running. Annual production value perhaps 2-4 million obols equivalent.
- Vroëszka doesn't receive obols directly—they trade metal for food, finished goods, and currencies for external trade.

**Cash flow out (allocation rather than spending):**
- Member allocations: 8,000 members receive food, housing, goods according to position (50-150 obols equivalent monthly)
- Terminal operations
- Defense (Elder Dravötsvel's faction)
- External factors at factorias
- Factional reserves for dormant periods

**Decision-making:** The seven-elder council theoretically governs. Each elder controls essential resources; each can threaten disruption; each knows the others face the same constraints. The result is equilibrium through mutual deterrence. Czenovaë could break deadlocks but speaks rarely. Her intervention in the schism that created Sztoavel—legitimizing rebels the council authorized military action against—demonstrated that her judgment might contradict consensus.

**Distinctive feature:** Terminal schedules create boom-bust cycles neither house controls. When a terminal runs, the controlling faction enjoys sudden wealth; when it falls dormant, that wealth evaporates. Houses employ watchers whose sole function is detecting signs of reactivation.

---

### Czernaël Dock Precinct

**What it does:** Controls the harbor where all food imports arrive. This is existential leverage—Asovoë cannot feed itself.

**Cash flow in:**
- Docking fees, handling fees, storage fees
- Brokerage: Czernaël intermediates transactions between feuding houses

**Revenue equivalent:** Perhaps 1-2 million obols annually in service value.

**Cash flow out:**
- Harbor maintenance
- Member allocations (~5,000 members)
- Ship operations

**Decision-making:** Czernaël is "democratic" by Asovoë standards—leadership selected by membership vote. The Rezhda (assembly) meets to decide major questions. In practice, operational necessity concentrates authority in the Harbormaster and senior captains.

**Distinctive feature:** Czernaël cannot take sides in inter-house feuds. Their leverage (food control) is also their vulnerability (everyone depends on them). Neutrality is survival strategy.

---

### The Chancery (House Lavoëran)

**What it does:** Legal archives, arbitration, dispute resolution that prevents feuds from escalating to city-wide war.

**Cash flow in:**
- Filing fees: 50,000-100,000 obols annually
- Arbitration fees
- Research fees (access to precedent archives)

**Annual revenue:** Perhaps 150,000-250,000 obols equivalent.

**Cash flow out:**
- Recorder salaries
- Chancellor support and security
- Archive maintenance

**Decision-making:** The Chancellor holds ultimate authority to interpret precedent, prohibit specific acts during hostilities, and mandate dispute resolution. Chancellors serve until death—which, given Avouvar longevity, means centuries.

**Distinctive feature:** The Chancellor's veto power. Authority to prohibit specific acts exists because some acts would damage victor and vanquished equally—attacks on autofactory terminals, for instance.

## Household Profiles

### Household 14: Czevelan Vroëszka, Smelter Worker (House Allocation)

**Profile:** Avouvar male, 120 years old. Single (currently—three previous partnerships, all ended). Smelter worker in Terminal Seven.

**Monthly Allocation:** 80 obols equivalent in goods

| Category | Allocation |
|----------|-----------|
| Housing | House-provided |
| Food | House-provided (communal meals) |
| Clothing | House-provided |
| Personal allocation | 20-30 obols equivalent |
| Terminal bonus (when running) | Variable |

**Budget Balance:** The concept doesn't apply. Czevelan doesn't earn wages; he receives allocation. When his terminal runs, his faction has resources; when dormant, everyone tightens.

**Possessions:**
- Personal quarters in house compound
- Work equipment (house-provided)
- Personal effects accumulated over 120 years
- Family heirlooms from previous generations
- Small collection of external goods (purchased at factorias)

**Economic Anxieties:**
- Terminal Seven has been dormant for three years
- His faction (Elder Sztravoëtsin's) is weakening
- The younger factions are ambitious
- He remembers better times—his parents' generation had more to allocate
- Not about survival—house provides that—but about status, faction position, the slow decline

### Household 15: Elder Sztravaë Vroëszka, Faction Leader (House Elite)

**Profile:** Avouvar female, 640 years old. Widowed (husband died 200 years ago). Controls one of seven major factions within House Vroëszka. Grandmother to perhaps 400 living descendants.

**Monthly Allocation:** Unlimited draw from faction resources

| Category | Usage |
|----------|-------|
| Personal compound | Faction-provided |
| Staff | 20+ servants |
| Entertainment | Extensive |
| Gifts and obligations | Substantial |
| Factional operations | Primary use of resources |

**Budget Balance:** Irrelevant at this level. Sztravaë's "wealth" is control over faction resources—perhaps 2,000 members' allocations, several terminals, accumulated goods and favors over centuries. She doesn't think in terms of personal budget.

**Possessions:**
- Faction compound (centuries of accumulation)
- Art and objects collected over 600 years
- Clothing from seven different eras
- Gifts from six Chancellors
- Personal archives (her memory requires documentation)

**Economic Anxieties:**
- Terminal schedules—her terminals have been running well, but that can change
- Factional balance—two of the other six elders are allied against her
- The young—they want change, don't remember the famines of 300 years ago
- Czenovaë—the Matriarch's occasional interventions are unpredictable
- Memory—she's begun keeping more notes; some centuries blur together

## Consistency Requirements

- Asovoë houses don't hold obols personally—external trade generates currency houses hold collectively
- Terminal schedules create boom-bust cycles no one controls
- Czernaël controls all food imports—cannot take sides in feuds
- The Chancellor can prohibit specific acts during hostilities
- Elders plan on century timescales; immortality transforms economic thinking
