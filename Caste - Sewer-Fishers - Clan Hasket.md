# Clan Hasket: The Big-Shoulder Sewer-Fishers

> **Location:** Partially collapsed warehouse complex at bottom of Bright-Spire
> **Demographics:** ~20% pure-line sewer-fishers ("little cousins"), ~80% stevedore orphans ("big cousins")
> **Operations:** Aquaculture, freight labor, distillery
> **Motto:** "Clan first, coin last"

The Haskets prove that family transcends genetics.

## Important Members

**Matron Tasme Hasket** (Age 62, "Little Mother Tasme"): The last pure-line Hasket matriarch, standing barely 145 cm with typical sewer-fisher features. Maintains clan ledgers and shrine with methodical precision. Speaks rarely but controls resource allocation—water credits, still rations, work assignments—with absolute authority. Has an elaborate tattoo from ear to collarbone received from an Oracle Cult shamaness. Doesn't talk about it.

**"Uncle" Brakk Hasket** (Age 48, Stevedore Fostering): Dock foreman who leads freight crews, standing 202 cm with Redback stevedore build. Lost his right hand to chain snap, now wears a steel hook prosthetic. Despite not being blood family, his loyalty to Tasme is absolute—she saved him from Chain-Men execution after a toll dispute fifteen years ago.

**Voss Hasket** (Age 24, Little Cousin): Tasme's nephew and likely heir to clan leadership. Manages aquaculture operations in flooded lower Bright-Spire, maintaining delicate balance of salinity and nutrition for mussel beds. Mathematical intuitions about fluid dynamics exceed typical sewer-fisher capabilities.

**Kenna Hasket-Sarn** (Age 31, Little Cousin): Maintains marriage ties with deep-dwelling Sarns. Monthly trips coordinating mussel harvests with deep-tunnel water flows provide crucial intelligence about below conditions.

**Rem Hasket** (Age 35, Big Cousin): Brakk's primary lieutenant, an Ironbone variant standing nearly 2.4 meters. Coordinates with Pipefitters Union, holding precious union card providing strike protection. His presence alone prevents most physical confrontations.

**Jek and Toll Hasket** (Ages 19 and 17, Big Cousins): Brothers from Springheel stock, more compact than other stevedores but with superior spatial reasoning. Handle precision loading requiring complex weight distribution understanding.

**"Little" Mika Hasket** (Age 14, Little Cousin): Shows promising mathematical intuitions and maintains clan still operations, understanding fermentation chemistry through pattern recognition.

**Dora Hasket** (Age 41, Big Cousin): Manages interface with Yen Tam Beneficial Society. Her stevedore heritage reduces traditional prejudice against sewer-fisher financial dealings. Maintains clan informal banking.

## Family Business and Relationships

The Haskets operate three interconnected businesses from lower Bright-Spire. Aquaculture occupies six flooded sub-basement levels where engineered mussels and eels grow in carefully maintained pools. Little cousins manage water chemistry through intuition, adjusting salinity and nutrient flows. Production feeds clan members and generates trade surplus.

Freight operations employ mostly big cousins, handling loads too heavy for standard dock workers. They specialize in materials requiring both strength and care—industrial machinery moved without damage, chemical containers requiring steady handling. The Salvage Guild pays premium rates.

The still produces industrial alcohol from Loak potatoes and dock-swept barley, requiring precise temperature control little cousins manage through mathematical intuition. Product serves multiple markets: Pipefitters use it as valve de-icer, Yen Tam gambling dens serve it as rotgut, various groups employ it as solvent. Location in contested space between Pipefitter and Guild territories makes it politically neutral ground.

The armory hidden in Hasket territory represents communal sewer-fisher insurance. Various clans store firearms in Hasket-controlled lockboxes, understanding any use would trigger massive Hanged Men retaliation. The weapons exist as final deterrent, their presence known but never acknowledged. Brakk controls access.

## Living Situation and Idiosyncrasies

The clan occupies a partially collapsed warehouse complex where structural damage from the drainage-vault disaster created a maze of interconnected spaces. The building's subsided foundation means ground level sits below water table, requiring constant pump operations. This suits the clan—flooded lower levels provide aquaculture space while upper levels house population.

Living arrangements reflect demographic split without formal separation. Little cousins cluster near mechanical spaces where pumps need constant attention. Big cousins occupy a converted loading floor with ceiling height accommodating their frames. Common areas—particularly communal kitchen and shrine room—see both groups mixing freely.

The dietary divide has dissolved through necessity. Both eat from the same pot, sharing eel stew and barley flatbread. Traditional sewer-fisher aquaculture diet expanded to include dock-worker staples like dried fruit and hardtack, creating hybrid cuisine reflecting mixed heritage.

Daily routine accommodates different biological needs. Little cousins handle night maintenance with superior low-light vision. Big cousins work daylight dock shifts where strength commands premium wages. Evening brings convergence, sharing meals and coordinating operations.

Education differs between groups. Little cousins learn traditional mathematics through pattern recognition. Big cousins receive practical training, but both Tasme and Brakk insist they learn something of mathematical intuition. None grasp it, but clan rule: they are all the same, no matter what genes say.

The shrine holds particular significance as unifying space. Beyond standard icons, it maintains memorial tablets for three generations lost in drainage collapse. Both groups add names of their own dead, creating genealogical record encompassing both bloodlines.
