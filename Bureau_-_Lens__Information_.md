## **Theological and Political Tensions**

### **The Charter and Dominion**

The central political tension within the Bureauâ€”and the Empireâ€”concerns the City-of-Glass Charter and what kind of Dominion is appropriate. This debate operates somewhere between legal doctrine and theology, with profound implications for how information is controlled.

### **The Eighth Oracle Question**

The Lattice's work with the Jargon raises the fundamental question: Is approaching Technical Caste mathematics repeating the Eighth Oracle's error? The Eighth Oracle commanded the Empire to create a naked singularity, which led to the Interdict. The Technical Castes refused, were sterilized, and their mathematical language was suppressed.

Now the Lattice approaches that same language, claiming their cryptographic frameworks keep them safe. They insist they're not corrupted, not approaching transcendence, not risking another catastrophe. The conservatives fear they're wrong. The liberals hope they're right.
