# The Galen Family

> **Type:** Infrastructure family
> **Location:** Ridge-Line Scriptoria, Penthouse Block (Ridge)
> **Function:** Information monopoly (notary services, intelligence)
> **Key asset:** Grey Sheet access, cryptographic services

The Galen Family runs Ridge-Line Scriptoria, operating as licensed notaries and White Sheet reprinters publicly, while managing a more lucrative grey-market intelligence operation.

## The Dual Operation

**Public face (Leran Galen):**
- Printing and notarization
- Licensed White Sheet reprinting
- Respectable businessman role

**Intelligence operation (Nativa Galen):**
- Cryptographic contractor for Bureau of the Lens
- Provides encryption services to anyone in the Rotten Quarter who needs secure communications
- **Reads those communications** as unavoidable consequence of providing the service
- Receives classified Grey Sheet from the Lens
- Sells carefully redacted excerpts to interested parties

## The Grey Sheet System

The Bureau of the Lens tolerates Nativa's intelligence distribution because it helps stabilize district politics through back-channel communication.

**The system works:**
- Bureaus can communicate with district powers without official statements
- District powers receive intelligence through controlled channels
- Everyone maintains useful fictions
- The Ridge remains stable enough to avoid requiring intervention

## Key Members

**Leran Galen** — Husband. Public-facing businessman. Handles printing and notarization.

**Nativa Galen** — Wife. The actual power. Cryptographic contractor, Grey Sheet distributor, intelligence broker.

## Family Connections

**Sera Fen-Galen** (Night Watch Captain for the Hanged Men) comes from this family—married to Ruk Fen (Luka Fen's younger brother). The connection demonstrates how the Galens extend their information network even into the Middens' power structure.

Sera operates as a triple agent (Galens, Fens, and Bureau of the Lens Asset LS-4-113), embodying the family's approach: serve everyone and no one, provide usefulness to multiple parties, maintain value through calculated betrayals.

## Interdependence

The Galens cannot operate alone:
- **Need Val water** for printing operations
- **Need Marzen electricity** for printing operations
- **Provide intelligence** that helps both families anticipate district changes
- **Provide communication** enabling the three-family balance to function

## Position in District

Unlike the Val and Marzen monopolies based on physical infrastructure, the Galen monopoly is based on:
- Trust (customers believe encryption is secure)
- Access (Grey Sheet clearance is irreplaceable)
- Networks (relationships across multiple organizations)

They maintain position through usefulness rather than violence—but their usefulness includes knowing things that could destroy others if revealed.
