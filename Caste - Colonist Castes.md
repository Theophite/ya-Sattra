# Colonist Castes (Serrulata)

> **Location:** Ganat and surrounding territories
> **Common features:** Scaled cap, pointed ears, amber eyes
> **Relationship to Empire:** Distinct population with own social systems
> **Key social structure:** Kilit (hereditary debt/guild obligations)

The Serrulata are colonist castes adapted for Ganat's desert environment. They share common features—scaled cranium, pointed ears, amber eyes—but have diversified into distinct populations.

## Common Serrulata Features

- Scaled cap covering cranium
- Large, sharply pointed ears
- Broad, flat nose
- Thin lips
- Amber eyes (shade shifts with light exposure history)

## Sarruk (Adaptive Caste)

**Type specimen:** Yem Kel-Vaseth, 37, warehouse supervisor

**Key characteristic:** Phenotypic plasticity—bodies shift over time toward comfort in whatever conditions they face.

**Convergent appearance:** Every sarruk in an area looks similar not because they are related, but because they are all adapting to the same environment.

**Yem's history:**
- As child on agricultural plot: highly reflective skin, copper/bronze scales, pale eyes
- After 17 years in warehouses: reduced reflectivity, muted grey-brown scales, eyes adapted to oil lamps

**Palimpsest effect:** The body becomes a palimpsest. Look closely at Yem's hands and you can see the ghost of agricultural coloration beneath current warehouse phenotype.

**Irreversibility:** Adaptation cannot be easily reversed; relocating would begin new adaptation, but traces would remain.

## Nasif (Unknown Original Purpose)

**Type specimen:** Tekhel Dhal-Orin, 28, clerk

**Key characteristic:** No one knows what the nasif were originally engineered for. The purpose has been lost.

**Physical description:**
- Neck extending dramatically, raising head far above narrow shoulders
- Extremely thin, limbs attenuated, torso narrow
- Six foot two but seems taller due to proportions
- Moves carefully, aware neck is vulnerable

**Occupation:** Hereditary Debtor working off obligations his grandfather incurred. The kilit staffs every significant office in the Republic; members become most competent administrators in government.

**Personal qualities:** Conversations stall when he cannot find expected response. Social gatherings exhaust him. The elongated frame makes him strange to look at—he knows it, and the knowledge makes him stranger.

## Uliq ("The Firstborn")

**Type specimen:** Sekh Tero-Maleth, 53, goat herder

**Claim:** The uliq claim to be the prototype from which other Serrulata castes derived.

**Physical description:**
- Skull distended, housing enlarged brain case with extensive sinus cavities for cooling
- No external ears (reduce surface area vulnerable to sun/sand)
- Lipless mouth (minimize moisture loss)
- Mauve-grey skin reflecting solar radiation
- Eyes nearly black, sclera invisible

**Communication:** Relies more on voice and gesture than facial expression—face does not move the way other faces move.

**Lifestyle:** Deep desert pastoralists. Fifty-three years walking the same territory his father walked, and his father's father.

## Draëthen (Synchronized Labor)

**Type specimen:** Grey-Patch, 31, weaver

**Key characteristic:** Engineered for synchronized labor. They model systems with such fidelity that separate individuals working the same problem reach identical conclusions.

**Appearance:** Most baseline-adjacent of refugee castes. Average height, average build, coloring within normal ranges.

**Subtle differences:**
- Movements too regular—stride length precisely matched step to step
- Falls into rhythms so exact observers can predict each motion
- Face shifts expression without transition: neutral, then smiling, intermediate stages absent

**Work:** In weaving collective, workers produce patterns of startling consistency—each section matching others perfectly, seams invisible. They do not coordinate consciously; their minds converge on the same solution.

**Experience:** Következ—the next thing, the item that must be completed before anything else can exist. The satisfaction is deep and continuous.

**Community:** Lives in compound with forty-three others. They wear identical clothing, cut hair identically, answer to distinguishing features (Grey-Patch, Crooked-Thumb). Decisions emerge through gradual alignment—not discussion but adjustment, each mind shifting toward consensus.
