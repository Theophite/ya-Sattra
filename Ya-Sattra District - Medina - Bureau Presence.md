# Bureau Presence in the Medina

> **Character:** Sub-offices, inspection stations, accumulated infrastructure of governance
> **Function:** Processing paperwork that makes Imperial life technically legal
> **Reality:** Selective enforcement is the actual system

The Bureaus maintain presence not through grand ministries (those occupy the Fourth Whorl) but through sub-offices distributing across commercial territory.

## Bureau of the Scale (Licensing)

**~12 licensing offices throughout the Medina:**
- Office of Premises Certification (building occupancy)
- Office of Professional Standing (non-guild trades—waiting lists extend years)
- Office of Contract Registration (agreements between parties)

**Character:** Small, cramped, staffed by junior clerks. Bronze plaque bearing Bureau seal. Waiting areas fill by mid-morning.

**Private notaries** cluster nearby, offering services the Bureau technically provides but practically cannot deliver in reasonable time. Fees exceed Bureau rates; turnaround is hours rather than months.

## Minor Aureates and Facilitation

Minor Aureates whose patents have become worthless but whose status retains procedural value:
- Families holding rights to authentication protocols for systems no one uses
- Certification requirements for technologies that no longer exist

**They survive by selling facilitation:**
- Knowing which office actually handles relevant paperwork
- Which clerk responds to which approaches
- Which procedures can be expedited

**Value:** Not bribery (though that occurs) but navigating complexity that defeats outsiders.

## Bureau of the Rod (Patents)

Inspection stations theoretically monitoring patent compliance for technologies in active use.

**Reality:** Inspection follows patterns everyone understands:
- Regular visits to established businesses that pay expected fees
- Documented violations generating revenue without disrupting operations
- Careful avoidance of discoveries requiring responses no one wants

**Inspectors know which equipment violates which patents. Businesses know inspectors know. Everyone maintains the fiction that inspection constitutes enforcement rather than taxation.**

Rod presence intensifies near Archive Gate (salvage entering Medina commerce).

## Bureau of the Lens (Censorship)

Censorship offices responsible for approving:
- Public communications
- Printed materials
- Commercial speech

**Requirements are comprehensive. Enforcement is selective.**

Most Medina commerce proceeds without Lens approval because:
- Seeking approval would be slower than conducting business
- Lens lacks resources to monitor everything

**Focus areas:** Materials circulating widely, communications influencing political opinion, content that might embarrass the Bureau.

A merchant's price list does not require approval. A pamphlet criticizing Bureau policy would never receive it. Everything between exists in gray space.

## Bureau of the Sword (Police)

Police stations staffed by **gendarmes** (not soldiers—the Sword's actual military guards the Inner City).

**Typical station:** ~30 gendarmes covering several blocks. Patrol in pairs.

**Relationship with criminal enterprises:**
- Territories where gendarmes do not patrol without reason
- Incidents not investigated thoroughly
- Understandings keeping violence within acceptable limits

**Administrative functions:** Registering weapons, processing security permits, maintaining records on individuals attracting official attention.

## Bureau of the Creche (Caste Registration)

Registration offices handling caste documentation:
- Caste certification
- Lineage documentation
- Various attestations Imperial life requires

**Comprehensive in theory, incomplete in practice:**
- Records extend back millennia for established families
- Gaps, losses, and complexity create uncertainties
- Petitioners with incomplete documentation face delays measured in months

## Bureau of the Coin (Revenue)

Revenue stations collecting taxes and fees. **Unlike other Bureau presences: prominent and well-staffed.**

**Multiple revenue streams:**
- Commercial licensing fees
- Transaction taxes on high-value commerce
- Various assessments Coin has accumulated authority to collect

**Tax farmers:** Private collectors purchasing right to collect in specific areas. Pay Coin a guaranteed sum, keep whatever additional revenue they extract. Universally resented, occasionally necessary.
