# Penthouse Block

> **Type:** Neighborhood (elite residential)
> **Location:** Tiers 573-600, separated by tidal canal
> **Population:** Infrastructure families, senior Pipefitters, successful craftspeople
> **Access:** Penthouse Span rope bridge, Cho gondola from Middens

Penthouse Block comprises the arcology's uppermost floors, now physically separated from the main Ridge by a tidal canal running south through the gap where sections settled apart.

## Access and Isolation

Access requires crossing the **Penthouse Span**—a rope bridge substantial enough for regular use but emphasizing the Block's semi-isolation.

Despite the separation, the Block isn't a remote fortress:
- The bridge sees regular traffic
- The canal is only twenty meters wide at high tide
- The **Cho gondola** arrives here directly from Lantern-Heaps in the Middens

## The Communal Garden

The garden provides visual relief from stone and metal—terraced beds growing vegetables alongside decorative plants.

**This is a luxury** distinguishing Penthouse Block from the rest of the Rotten Quarter. Both decorative pleasure AND practical food production.

## Residents

**The Infrastructure Families:**
- Val family (water control)
- Marzen family (electrical distribution)
- Galen family (intelligence operations)

**Also housed here:**
- Most successful Arcade craftspeople who've earned premium housing
- Senior Pipefitters and community organization officials

## The Yen Tam Restaurant

The Yen Tam Beneficial Society maintains an upscale restaurant and bar serving ya-Tsatsan specialties. This demonstrates how the Ridge's relative respectability allows organizations with criminal roots elsewhere to operate conventionally here.

Cooking smells and conversation sounds emanate from the establishment—one of the few genuine luxuries on the Ridge.

## Marriage Patterns

Qirren Val's arranged marriage to Dara Marzen demonstrates Ridge pragmatism:
- Both are gay
- Both need heirs
- Both understand their families require alliances and succession

They'll marry, have children, and maintain the Ridge's stability through another generation. Love is beside the point; mutual respect and shared duty provide sufficient foundation.

This pragmatism extends beyond the elite families—most Ridge marriages are practical arrangements based on compatible businesses, complementary skills, or advantageous family connections.

## Physical Character

Even here, the underlying wrongness persists:
- Ceilings slope unexpectedly
- Corridors turn at angles that don't quite make sense
- Everything has been adapted, but adaptation can only do so much

The family homes show investment in proper finishing—good tile work, matched furniture, reliable systems. But the rotated arcology architecture remains visible beneath the modifications.
