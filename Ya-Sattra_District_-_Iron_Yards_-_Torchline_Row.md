# Torchline Row

> **Type:** Commercial strip
> **Location:** Elevated limestone ridge above Iron Yards industrial floor
> **Function:** Services, commerce, vice establishments
> **Character:** Constant spark rain; fire-resistant construction required

The limestone ridge was carved out millennia ago, its stone fed into arcology construction. Now the quarried terraces serve as foundation for the district's commercial strip—elevated just enough above the harbor's industrial sprawl to catch what passes for fresh air.

The constant rain of welding sparks from cutting operations has given the ridge its name.

## Key Establishments

### Den Vesh Salvage Assessment

**Den Vesh** — Elderly, blind (radiation cataracts). Claims he can still perceive the Cherenkov glow from hot materials.

**Function:** Assesses materials too dangerous for Guild certification—radiation-tagged salvage that would trigger quarantine if examined through official channels.

**Value:** Willing to handle what others won't touch. Prices reflect risk premium.

**Protection:** Pays Yen Tam, Union, AND Iron Yards Boys—none decide he's more valuable as example than revenue.

### Seru and Sari Protective Tailoring

**Seru and Sari** — Twin sisters, near-baseline with enhanced color vision. 47, unmarried, refused every offer to expand.

**Products:** Lead-thread aprons, radiation-resistant coveralls, spark-protective masks with mica lenses.

**Reputation:** Every salvage worker who wants to survive owns something from this shop.

**Neutrality:** Identical quality to all customers regardless of faction. A Chain-Man's apron is sewn with same care as a Pipefitter's.

### Keth Varemgiren's Operations

**Keth Varemgiren** — 61, near-baseline, claims Companion Guild training (unconfirmed), 30 years in Iron Yards.

**The Cask and Flux (pharmacy):**
- Painkillers diluted just enough to avoid narcotic classification
- Radiation ointments (possibly repackaged industrial sealant)
- Stimulants keeping workers functional through double shifts

**The Coil-Bone Clinic:**
- Setting fractures with improvised spring-loaded braces
- Maintaining respirators for toxic environments
- Replacing joints with salvaged mechanical components

**Credential:** Workers who visit generally return to work.

**Assistant Mora** — Young orevet, vision too degraded for precision work but can identify infection by heat signatures.

### Venn Rigging and Rope

**Clan Venn** (sewer-fishers) — Technical operation with baseline fosterlings as front-of-house.

**Keras Venn** (45) — Master ropemaker, oversees operations, personally tests every splice.

**Dek and Rol Venn** — Foster twins (near-baseline), handle customer interactions requiring baseline appearance.

**Products:** Everything from surgical sutures to massive dock cables. Mathematical patterns optimize strength beyond materials alone.

**Reputation:** When gantries fail or bridges snap, investigations never trace back to Venn cable.

### Yellow Glass Bathhouse

**Yellow-tinted glass** from somewhere no one remembers gave the establishment its name.

**Services:**
- Communal baths (coal-fired boilers)
- Sleeping areas (40 obols/night)
- Decontamination facilities for radiation exposure

**Secondary function:** Money laundering through inflated maintenance costs under Yen Tam accounting oversight.

### Yen Tam Seven Branches

**Gambling follows ya-Tsatsan style:** Seven-sided dice, Testament suit cards, seven-fold betting structures.

**Character:** Serious, quiet. Religious observance disguised as (or simultaneous with) gambling.

**Shrine:** Seven brass plates inscribed with Jargon arranged in open octagon; eighth point is mirror reflecting room.

### Other Establishments

**The Burned Hand** — Primary tavern. Three connected buildings, multiple entrances (useful for those avoiding observation). Run by **Kella Varn** (lost left arm to cutting accident).

**The Char** — Cheap food. **Grandmother Shen** produces rice porridge, pickled vegetables, whatever protein she found cheap. 40 years in the Yards.

**Morrow's Tools** — Hand equipment sales and rental. **Cass Morrow**'s credit terms more generous than Yen Tams', creating periodic tension.

**The Pipe and Gauge** — Pipefitters bar/informal union hall. **Steward Kollis** manages.

**Kella's Bunkhouse** — Temporary housing (15 obols/night). Run by **Kella Marsh**. Basic decontamination facilities. Reports unusual activity to Union.
