# Medina Businesses Directory

Named establishments for specific scene locations.

## Guild Shops

### The House of Venn (Booksellers' Row)
- **Proprietor:** Calis Venn (58, F, near-baseline)—11th generation, will pass to daughter Miris
- **Specialty:** Legal texts—Scale precedents, Bureau procedural manuals, contract templates
- **Inventory:** ~3,000 volumes, hand-copied by licensed scriptoriums
- **Reputation:** Personally verifies that legal citations match official records
- **Prices:** Modest volume 2 obols; rare precedent compilations 20+

### Torel's Copper (Coppersmith Lane)
- **Proprietor:** Dav Torel (67, M, near-baseline)—~200th Torel in this space
- **Location:** Same for 4,000+ years
- **Products:** Utilitarian copperware (pots, basins, pipes, fittings)
- **Staff:** 3 journeymen, 2 apprentices
- **Prices:** Guild-fixed (cooking pot = 1.5 obols regardless of shop)
- **Competition:** Quality and relationship, not price

### The Lens and Frame (Alley of Lenses)
- **Proprietor:** Sera Monn (45, F, near-baseline)—inherited through marriage
- **Note:** Cannot grind lenses herself; employs 2 journeymen who can
- **Products:** Reading glasses, magnifying instruments, replacement lenses
- **Expertise:** Matching customers with appropriate optics
- **Prices:** Basic reading glass 3 obols; precision instruments 50+

### Maren's Clothiers (Clothiers' Quarter)
- **Proprietor:** Tev Maren (52, M, near-baseline)—master member, licenses 3 contractors
- **Staff:** ~40 workers (cutting, sewing, finishing)
- **Products:** Ready-made garments for middle-class—clerk's robes, merchant's coats
- **Prices:** Clerk's robe 4 obols; merchant's coat 8 obols
- **Advantage:** Faster delivery and better fit than neighbors

## Teahouses

### The Clerk's Rest (near Fourth Whorl)
- **Proprietor:** Lira Doshen (38, F, near-baseline)—inherited from uncle
- **Layout:** Common room (coca tea, quarter obol) + private rooms (Westerner tea available)
- **Clientele:** Junior bureaucrats seeking space for afternoon meetings
- **Private rooms:** Rent by hour, valued for discretion

### Kem's (near Grain Exchange)
- **Proprietor:** Kem Darros (61, M, near-baseline)—32 years same corner
- **Type:** Standing-room only
- **Product:** Coca tea from Ganati supplier
- **Price:** One-eighth obol (cheapest with seating)
- **Character:** Quick transactions; knows regulars by face

### The Westerner's Rest
- **Proprietor:** Avira es-Dorran (44, F, near-baseline)—minor Aureate
- **Purpose:** Be seen drinking expensive tea
- **Westerner tea:** 5 obols per pot
- **Clientele:** Merchants cultivating sophistication, facilitators meeting clients

## Bars

### The Burnt Anchor (waterfront)
- **Proprietor:** Ghent Pol (55, M, near-baseline)—former dock worker (back damage)
- **Character:** Rough—wooden benches, sawdust floors
- **House beer:** One-eighth obol, brewed in cellar
- **Brandy:** Uncertain provenance, strong and cheap
- **Credit:** Extended to regulars, collected when paid

### Mira's (Clothiers' Quarter)
- **Proprietor:** Mira Tessik (48, F, near-baseline)
- **Character:** Comfortable—proper seating, good lighting, food available
- **Beer:** Licensed brewery product, half obol
- **Brandy:** Imported, measured cups
- **Function:** Business negotiations, guild politics

### The Final Draft (near Archive Gate)
- **Proprietor:** Jak Sennel (39, M, near-baseline)
- **Clientele:** Rotten Quarter workers wanting respectable drinking
- **Prices:** Higher than Rotten Quarter, lower than deeper Medina
- **Policy:** No credit, no questions about where money comes from

## Food Vendors

### Tessik's Noodles (near Grain Exchange)
- **Proprietor:** Ren Tessik (34, F, near-baseline)—recipes from mother, grandmother, ~80 years
- **Menu:** Noodles with fish, vegetables, or both
- **Price:** Quarter obol per bowl
- **Format:** No seating; eat standing or carry away

### The Marru House
- **Proprietor:** Coll Verran (62, M, near-baseline)—~200 years same location
- **Product:** Fermented fish sauce (marru) aging in ceramic vessels
- **Also:** Dried fish, preserved vegetables
- **Price:** Household vessel half obol, lasts ~month

### Doshan's Cart
- **Proprietor:** Vel Doshan (28, M, near-baseline)—unlicensed
- **Product:** Grilled goat on skewers, family seasoning recipe
- **Price:** One-eighth obol per skewer
- **Movement:** Follows crowds, avoids guild enforcers

## Professional Services

### The Facilitator's Office (near Scale offices)
- **Proprietor:** Peyor es-Talin (51, M, near-baseline)—minor Aureate, worthless patents
- **Service:** Navigation through bureaucratic complexity
- **Fees:** 2 obols (simple guidance) to substantial retainers

### Korral and Daughter (near Many's countinghouse)
- **Principal:** Tev Korral (64, M, near-baseline)—Scale certification
- **Daughter:** Lessa (31)—handles client interaction
- **Services:** Witness contracts, authenticate documents
- **Turnaround:** Hours (not months)
- **Volume:** ~30 transactions daily

### The Translation House
- **Principal:** Mira Venn (48, F, near-baseline)
- **Staff:** 6 translators (Imperial Common, Ganati, ya-Tsatsan, Akama pidgin)
- **Avouvar translation:** Available irregularly
- **Also:** Scribing for customers who cannot write

## Specialty Retailers

### Ganati Provisions (Ganati enclave)
- **Proprietor:** Yela Khen-Oris (38, F, Serrulata)
- **Imports:** Maguey-silk textiles, Republican copper work, preserved foods, coca leaves
- **Clientele:** Serrulata residents + Imperial customers wanting Republican products
- **Prices:** Transport costs + cross-border complexity justify markup

### The Archive Salvage Office (near Archive Gate)
- **Proprietor:** Dov Sennek (49, M, near-baseline)—Salvage Guild certified
- **Products:** Reconditioned archaeotech with official assessment
- **Expertise:** Matching customers with what they actually need
- **Policy:** Turns away customers he suspects would attract Lens attention

### Sacred Implements
- **Proprietor:** Keth Voral (55, F, near-baseline)
- **Products:** Religious goods for Orthodox Oracle households AND Penitent parishes
- **Neutrality:** Serves both faiths without favoring either
