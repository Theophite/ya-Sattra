# The Salvage Guild

> **Type:** Trade monopoly
> **Location:** Salvage Dome (Iron Yards)
> **Function:** Controls all salvage assessment and sale in ya-Sattra
> **Structure:** 147 fixed master memberships

The Salvage Guild holds a charter granted during emergency reconstruction following the arcology collapse—a document whose age gives it legal weight newer institutions cannot challenge.

## The Charter

**Emergency reconstruction exemption:** The charter exempts Guild operations from patent enforcement for "emergency reconstruction purposes."

That the emergency ended centuries ago has not diminished the exemption's value.

## The 147 Masters

Fixed by charter, the master memberships create an aristocracy within the salvage economy:

**Range:**
- Industrial magnates controlling entire material streams
- Marginal operators renting floor access to contractors

**Working masters:** Perhaps 60 of the 147 actively manage operations and appear daily. The wealthiest operate through representatives while maintaining estates on the Ridge or beyond.

**Inheritance:** Membership can be inherited but must be validated through demonstrating "continuous engagement in salvage operations"—a requirement preventing pure speculation while allowing flexibility. Some masters satisfy it through owning a single cutting torch operated by contractors paying for the privilege.

## The Contractor Economy

The real salvage economy operates through thousands of contractors working under master licenses.

**Arrangements vary:**
- Exploitation: Contractors paying 60% of revenues for license use
- Genuine partnerships: Masters provide capital and political protection

**Guild blindness:** The Guild maintains careful blindness about contractor operations—allowing masters to claim ignorance about patent violations or dangerous practices.

## Fee Structure

| Fee | Rate | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| **Auction** | 18% | Base transaction fee |
| **Authentication** | 2% | Verification |
| **Floor space** | 1-3% | Variable |
| **Security** | 1% | |
| **"Charter maintenance"** | 2% | |
| **Hazardous/discrete** | Variable | Additional charges |

**Total can reach 30%** for questionable archaeotech.

## Credit System

The Guild operates its own credit system—necessary because formal banks won't finance potentially illegal salvage operations.

**Interest rate:** 3% monthly, compounding.

**The trap:**
- Masters can borrow against future sales
- Contractors cannot
- Workers wanting equipment must finance through Yen Tams or Guild loan sharks
- Even successful salvagers rarely accumulate capital
- Those saving for membership find positions rarely available
- When available, prices require borrowing to afford

## Relationship with Other Powers

**With Pipefitters Union:** The Guild controls sales; the Union controls labor. Neither can destroy the other without destroying the economy.

**With Yen Tam Society:** Parallel exploitation systems. The Guild takes fees; the Yen Tams take interest. Workers are trapped between both.

**With Iron Yards Boys:** Tolerated extraction. The Guild pays protection rather than fight the Hanged Men.
