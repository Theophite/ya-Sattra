# The Val Family

> **Type:** Infrastructure family
> **Location:** Val Waterworks, Horizon Row (Ridge)
> **Function:** Water distribution monopoly
> **Key asset:** Deep standpipes to ancient underground reservoirs

The Val Family operates the Val Waterworks from a solid building on Horizon Row. Their monopoly derives from exclusive access to deep standpipes connecting to ancient underground reservoirs—the only source of reliably clean, fresh water in an area where easily accessible groundwater is contaminated by centuries of industrial runoff and bay water intrusion.

## Key Members

**Rina Val** — Manages business operations with precise efficiency. Handles customer relations, pricing, contract negotiations.

**Keram Val** — Maintains the physical systems with patient technical competence. Understands the ancient standpipes, the treatment systems, the distribution network.

**Qirren Val** — Son. Serves as Bureau of the Lens inspector for the Rotten Quarter.

## The Waterworks

The operation includes:
- Deep standpipes tapping ancient reservoirs
- Water treatment facilities
- Distribution network throughout the Ridge
- Public bathhouses providing hot water and cleaning services

**The bathhouses demonstrate reliability** through consistent temperature and cleanliness—showing customers what Val service means.

## Qirren Val: The Imperial Connection

Qirren serves as the only formal Imperial presence on the Ridge. His position gives the family:
- **Legitimacy:** Official recognition the other families lack
- **Intelligence:** Useful information about district conditions
- **Mediation capacity:** Can summon additional Lens resources if necessary

But Qirren's role is primarily **managing problems through relationships** rather than formal enforcement. He's a Ridge native who understands district realities rather than just official fictions.

His arranged marriage to Dara Marzen (both are gay, both need heirs) exemplifies Ridge pragmatism—alliance and succession matter more than personal preference.

## Interdependence

The Vals cannot operate alone:
- **Need Marzen electricity** to run their pumps
- **Need Galen intelligence** to anticipate district changes
- **Provide water** that Marzens need for reactor cooling
- **Provide water** that Galens need for printing operations

This mutual dependency creates stable balance maintained through generations of neighborly relationships.

## Position in District

The Vals see themselves as respectable mercantile operators:
- Legitimate businesspeople, not criminals
- Maintain position through technical competence and infrastructure control, not violence
- Understand their obligation to provide essential services reliably
- View themselves as equals to Medina Quarter merchants despite Rotten Quarter location

This self-perception shapes how they interact with customers, conduct negotiations, and expect to be treated.
