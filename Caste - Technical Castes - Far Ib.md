# Far Ib: Architecture and Function

> **Location:** Iystal Mountains
> **Function:** Former Technical Caste capital; current Archon facility
> **Population:** Seven Archons (various states), degrading clone servants
> **Status:** Slow collapse as virus continues and servants degrade

Far Ib represents what may have been the administrative or spiritual capital of Technical Caste civilization.

## Physical Structure

**Design Principles:**
- Architecture assumes non-human movement patterns
- Integrated biological support systems throughout
- Acoustic chambers for parallel information processing
- Direct integration points for Archon-architecture merger

**Current State:**
- Partially occupied by degraded infrastructure
- Maintained by diminishing clone servant populations
- Houses at least seven Archons in various states
- Contains quantum computing systems with external connections (notably Cloud Temple)

## The Archons of Far Ib

Seven Archons remain, designated by function rather than identity:

| Designation | State | Notes |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| **The Mouth** | Least modified | Capable of limited baseline communication; each word causes neural damage |
| **Master-Interior-Clarified** | Partially functional | Acknowledges external stimuli |
| **Master-Recursive-Ascending** | Minimal | Reduced to brain tissue in quantum suspension |
| **Master-Terminal-Violet** | Merged | Three Archons merged; one deceased for millennia |
| **Master-Prime-Forgotten** | Periodic | Attempts emergence from cryostasis |
| **Archon-Tertius** | Suspended | Form no longer recognizably human |
| **Archon-Septimus** | Integrated | Functions as living archive; merged into walls |

All are affected by the sterilization plague continuing its work at near-zero temperatures—slowing but not stopping cellular destruction.

## The Calculation

The Archons are engaged in a massive, continuous calculation attempting to "unmake" or disprove the Eighth Testament's false mathematics.

This is not conventional problem-solving but their existence in a state where the false Testament creates continuous mathematical dissonance in their consciousness.

**Observations:**
- The calculation may not have a definable end state
- Progress occurs at increasingly slow rates due to neural degradation
- Success would require not just proof but implementation of that proof
- The Archons may no longer remember the original purpose

Each Archon embodies a single Arbitrary Truth, generating axioms that contribute to the collective proof. The collaborative nature suggests the Truths form a unified system even if no single consciousness can apprehend that unity.

## Support Infrastructure

### Clone Servant Degradation

The Archons are maintained by clone servants showing severe generational deterioration:

- Loss of literacy (approximately generation Sev-Sev-Pri-Zero-Zero)
- Accumulating physical deformities
- Retention of behavioral patterns without understanding
- Critical maintenance tasks abandoned through knowledge loss

The servants perform ritualistic maintenance without comprehension, following genetic behavioral patterns that preserve basic functionality while losing all context.

### The Virus Persistence

The sterilization plague remains active, replicating at extremely reduced rates due to cold:

- Neural death continues at microscopic rates
- Repair mechanisms operate too slowly to compensate
- Each generation of viral particles slightly less effective but still damaging
- No possibility of cure with available technology

This creates a race between the Archons' calculation and their biological dissolution.

## The Mouth

The Mouth represents a unique position—modified enough to interface with Far Ib's systems but retaining sufficient baseline architecture to attempt human communication.

**Communication characteristics:**
- Extreme compression of multidimensional concepts into linear speech
- Physical pain from maintaining ambiguous thought patterns
- Inability to accurately convey mathematical concepts in human language
- Multiple possible interpretations for every statement

Each act of translation causes damage to neural tissue, as baseline language requires cognitive patterns incompatible with mathematical consciousness.

## External Connections

### Quantum Entanglement Systems

Quantum computers linking Far Ib to external facilities (notably Cloud Temple):
- Entangled states maintained across significant distances
- Acoustic output systems translating quantum states to sound
- Original power requirements suggesting additional, now-missing functionality
- Preservation despite lack of understanding suggests robust design

### External Recruitment

The Mouth has initiated contact with external groups, particularly the Cloud Temple Band under Kelso Senna.

**Requested resources:**
- Mutterers for technical maintenance and interface functions
- Intelligence from ya-Sattra
- Specific technical components
- Human agents for tasks requiring baseline cognition

The relationship between these requests and the Archons' calculation remains unclear—possibly because the Mouth itself cannot accurately translate the need.

### Mutterer Integration

Acquisition of Mutterers suggests:
- Recognition that clone servants cannot maintain complex systems
- Attempt to restore technical capability through quantity if not quality
- Possible interface between Archon consciousness and Mutterer optimization
- Acceptance of uncontrolled optimization as preferable to system failure

## Threat Assessment

**Immediate Risks:**
- Cascade failure of Far Ib systems as servants and Archons degrade
- Uncontrolled Mutterer choirs causing infrastructure damage
- Misinterpretation of Archon communications leading to catastrophic actions
- Loss of quantum entanglement cutting last communication channel

**Long-term Concerns:**
- Complete failure of the Archons' calculation
- Successful calculation without implementation method
- Unknown consequences of Archon success
- Gradual loss of all Technical Caste knowledge as Mutterers degrade
