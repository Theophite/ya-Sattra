# The Salvage Dome

> **Type:** Pre-Interdict structure / Guild headquarters
> **Dimensions:** ~200 feet base, ~180 feet interior, 70-foot ceiling at apex
> **Function:** Salvage Guild operations, auction floor, assessment center
> **Character:** Bronze construction "somehow wrong" compared to modern work

The dome itself is pre-Interdict construction of unknown original purpose. It somehow survived both the arcology collapse and subsequent centuries of industrial use.

## Physical Structure

**Dimensions:**
- ~200 feet at base
- ~180 feet interior diameter
- 70-foot ceiling at apex
- Main floor reinforced to support massive machinery during auctions

**Materials:**
- Walls and floor are bronze
- Dark patina worn to bright gold-red in traffic paths
- Massive circular skylights (40 feet across) glazed with pre-Interdict material—slightly greenish, very strong
- Carved relief covers every surface: geometric patterns, figures in profile conducting transactions, text in script no longer readable

**The wrongness:** The bronze curves are somehow wrong compared to contemporary construction. Something about the proportions, the way light reflects, the acoustic properties—it doesn't match what modern builders produce.

## Vertical Hierarchy

Catwalks added in successive layers over centuries create social stratification:

| Level | Occupants |
|-------|-----------|
| **Uppermost** | Masters' offices |
| **Middle tiers** | Junior members |
| **Ground level** | Physical logistics |

## The Auction Floor

The auction floor operates on three-hour cycles:

| Time | Category | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Morning** | Bulk metals | High volume, lower margins |
| **Afternoon** | Complex machinery | Precision work, higher value |
| **Evening** | "Specialty items" | Less scrutiny required |

**Bidding:** Masters bid through coded hand signals visible from the catwalks.

**Unwritten rules:**
- No one underbids by more than 15% without expecting retaliation
- Certain masters have acknowledged spheres where others don't compete

## The Oracle Shrine

In the dome's eastern corner stands an Oracle shrine—modest installation serving crucial social function.

**Curate Varen Taasaren:**
- Spent thirty years as Rod inspector before taking orders
- Dual role (religious authority + former Bureau official) makes him ideal mediator between Guild and workforce
- Maintains shrine while running small congregation from Dockside Hospice
- Donations nominally support shrine but practically fund work with injured Pipefitters
- Creates informal welfare system supplementing Guild's inadequate pension

## Northern Annexes

Specialized facilities:
- **Radiation containment** for hot materials
- **Chemical processing** for hazardous salvage
- **Secure storage** for materials under legal review
- **Underground vaults** (senior masters only)—some haven't been opened in decades, contents forgotten

## The Pit

Circular depression near the dome's eastern side. Serves as decontamination pad where radiation-tagged salvage gets dumped from Lift-3.

Ground sealed with archaeotech polymer that nothing grows through.
