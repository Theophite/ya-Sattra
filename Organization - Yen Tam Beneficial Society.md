# The Yen Tam Beneficial Society

> **Type:** Criminal organization / mutual aid society
> **Location:** Converted warehouse on Torchline Row (Iron Yards)
> **Function:** Vice monopoly—gambling, drugs, loans, enforcement
> **Origin:** Ya-Tsatsan refugees (50 years ago, theological disputes)

The Yen Tam Beneficial Society fled ya-Tsatsa fifty years ago after theological disputes within the Testament Societies. They established themselves first as legitimate insurers before expanding into gambling, loans, and drug distribution.

## Organizational Structure

Mirrors ya-Tsatsan clan patterns:
- Extended family networks
- Clear hierarchies
- Elaborate gift-exchange obligations

**Presence announced by:** Ya-Tsatsan wind-chimes and subtle chemical smell clinging to anyone who enters.

## Near-Baseline Chauvinism

**Genuine belief, not just strategy:** They believe near-baseline humans represent the proper form of humanity; modified castes are useful tools but not true people.

**Manifestations:**
- Near-baseline customers receive better rates, faster service, more generous loan terms
- Gambling rooms maintain separate tables for "pure" and "dilute" players
- Drug distribution follows clear hierarchy—modified castes pay more for worse product

**The contradiction:** Yet the Society can't afford complete segregation. Too many protection fees come from modified workers, too many loans go to sewer-fisher clans, too many medical supplies get distributed through modified contractors.

**Result:** An organization practicing discrimination while depending on those it discriminates against.

## Interconnected Operations

The warehouse's modified architecture connects criminal operations:

| Operation | Connects To | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Gambling rooms** | Drug processing | High-stakes losers immediately purchase consolation |
| **Loan office** | Enforcement squad (basement) | Direct communication for collection assignments |
| **Yellow Glass Bathhouse** | Yen Tam accounting | Money laundering through inflated maintenance |

## Key Establishments

**Seven Branches (gambling):** Ya-Tsatsan style—seven-sided dice, Testament suit cards, seven-fold betting structures. Religious observance disguised as (or simultaneous with) gambling.

**Yellow Glass Bathhouse:** Communal baths, lodging (40 obols/night), decontamination. Money laundering operation.

## Relationship with Iron Yards Boys

**Cold war** where both organizations test boundaries while avoiding open conflict:

| Yen Tam Does | Purpose |
|--------------|---------|
| Pays tolls promptly | Avoids confrontation |
| Provides loans to individual enforcers | Creates leverage |
| Shares information on mutual enemies | Maintains usefulness |
| Maintains own enforcement squad | Prevents direct Hanged Men extortion of members |

**Enforcement squad:** Near-baseline toughs and desperate stevedores waiting for collection assignments.

## The Debt Cycle

The Society's most insidious product is the debt cycle:

1. Workers borrow to cover wage shortfalls
2. Work extra shifts to service loans
3. Damage bodies faster
4. Require more medical care (often from Yen Tam suppliers)
5. Need more borrowing

Cycle continues until workers are too broken to continue—at which point they fall to the Middens.
