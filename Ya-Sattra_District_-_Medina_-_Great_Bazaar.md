# The Great Bazaar

> **Extent:** ~1/3 of the Medina Quarter
> **Structure:** Covered souks, open plazas, semi-permanent stalls
> **Range:** Fourth Whorl transition to eastern approaches
> **Layers:** White market, Gray market, Black margins

The Great Bazaar is not a single market but a sprawling network—it begins wherever merchants set up goods and ends wherever they don't.

## White Market (Guild Commerce)

Licensed guild shops operating under ancient charters predating Bureau organization.

**Characteristics:**
- Seven-pointed star marking guild affiliation
- Fees paid to both guild and Bureau
- Complete legal protection
- Higher prices, consistent quality, honest weights

**Infrastructure:** Permanent locations—stone buildings with heavy shutters, warehouses for storage.

## Gray Market (Paperwork Pending)

Trades technically legal but bureaucratically complex.

**Example situation:** A merchant selling archaeotech components needs:
- Rod documentation confirming patent status
- Salvage Guild certification establishing origin
- Coin customs clearance calculating duties

Obtaining this legitimately might take months and cost more than the goods are worth.

**Solution:** Merchant with "paperwork pending" or selling components "requiring additional documentation" operates in gray space—tolerated as long as appropriate fees reach appropriate pockets.

**Infrastructure:** Semi-permanent stalls (wooden frames, canvas covers), locations shifting according to unofficial arrangement.

## Black Margins (Everyone Knows, No One Acknowledges)

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Stolen documents | "Historical documents" that happen to be Bureau records |
| Controlled substances | "Pharmaceutical preparations" requiring unauthorized ingredients |
| Forbidden items | Black Calculus fragments in lead-lined cases |
| Suppressed information | Data someone powerful wants hidden |

**How it works:** Back rooms, basement levels, private residences. Requires introduction, reputation, and mutual vulnerability.

## Foreign Commerce

### The Ganati Enclave
- **Location:** Three blocks near eastern approaches
- **Population:** ~5,000 permanent residents
- **Character:** Heat-responsive pigments shifting through propaganda slogans, sand-colored composites

**Trade mission:** Everyone knows it violates Imperial monopolies. Empire cannot afford to shut it down.

**Goods flow both directions:**
- Outbound: Furnace components, Imperial manufactures
- Inbound: Maguey-silk textiles, copper work, agricultural products

**Religious services:** Gridward-Approach parish—Penitent services in three languages.

### Sztoavel Imports (Asovoë Presence)
- **Location:** Narrow limestone building, side street, no sign
- **Manager:** Factor Eln (Avouvar—jet-black light-absorbing skin)

**Specialty:** "Things the Empire needs and can't manufacture"—specialty alloys requiring radiation, specific conditions, inherited infrastructure.

**The arrangement:** Factor Eln accommodates ya-Sattra's discretion requirements while maintaining paper trails that keep Bureau inspectors satisfied. Certain shipments contain exactly what documentation claims.

### Akama Traders
Rare in the Medina—perhaps a handful at any given time. Felt and leather marking steppe origin.

An Akama in the Medina is likely lost, looking for something specific, or accompanying cargo requiring personal attention.

### Ta-Kefyeh Presence
Subtle but significant. Symbiotic parasites controlling engineered clone hosts:
- Recognized as elite Imperial servants
- Respected for fierce defense of consent laws governing host-parasite relationships
- Visually disturbing to unfamiliar populations

The Medina's cosmopolitanism makes it more welcoming than districts where residents might mistake a ta-Kefyeh host for a corpse.
