## Core Principle: Sacred Labor

Ya-Don operates under fundamentally different principles from ya-Sattra. The Order of St. Akhmet controls the Furnaces through knowledge that cannot be extracted—it exists in human hands and minds, transmitted through apprenticeship. This gives labor absolute power in ways capitalism systematically prevents.

The religious framework shapes economic outcomes. Work is penitential labor demonstrating humanity's growing wisdom. Wages compress because theology demands it; the Forge-Master earning 700-850 obols monthly while journeymen earn 280-350 obols represents far less inequality than comparable capitalist enterprises.

The es-Etaie capitation arrangement exemplifies non-capitalist logic: one family pays religious tax for 40,000 workers, funding their freedom to worship without extracting corresponding labor.

**Historical parallel:** Guild socialism as theorized by G.D.H. Cole—worker-controlled enterprises operating through functional democracy—combined with medieval religious orders managing economic production.

## Major Institutions

### Order of St. Akhmet's Furnace

**What it does:** Primary labor union controlling Furnace operations. Approximately 40,000 members producing the only new complex technology in the Empire.

**Cash flow in:**
- Member dues: ~400,000 obols/month from 40,000 members
- es-Etaie capitation: ~1.6 million obols annually (family pays Church tax for all members)
- Production payments flow through Furnace Board negotiations

**Annual revenue:** ~6 million obols in dues and es-Etaie capitation combined.

**Cash flow out:**
- Member benefits (injury support, family assistance): ~1.5 million obols/year
- Shift Hall operations: ~1 million obols/year
- Religious observance: ~500,000 obols/year
- Training programs: ~800,000 obols/year
- Administrative staff: ~1 million obols/year
- Strike fund: accumulates during normal operations

**Decision-making:** The seven-member Council of Master Tenders governs. Each Master Tender represents a geographic section and is elected by workers. Council decisions require five of seven votes for major matters (strikes, negotiations), four of seven for routine business. The Forge-Master chairs but holds only one vote. The Order's Arch-Curate can invoke theological concerns to block actions violating Penitent principles.

**Distinctive feature:** The Order's absolute leverage. A strike shuts down the Empire's only source of new complex technology. This has happened twice in recorded history; both times the Empire capitulated within weeks.

---

### Covenant of Saint Aureline the Provider

**What it does:** Logistics cartel controlling all cargo movement into, out of, and through ya-Don. Approximately 35,000 members.

**Cash flow in:**
- Tariffs: 2-15% on all cargo (~2-4 million obols/year)
- Infrastructure fees: ~1 million obols/year
- Member dues: ~2.5 million obols/year

**Annual revenue:** Approximately 6-8 million obols.

**Cash flow out:**
- Infrastructure maintenance: ~2 million obols/year
- Staff and logistics workers: ~3 million obols/year
- Transit Chapel operations: ~200,000 obols/year
- Political operations: substantial but undocumented

**Decision-making:** The Covenant Master is elected by membership but practically selected from among senior Weighmasters who control tariff assessment. The twenty Weighmasters form an informal council that shapes policy through control of the bottleneck.

**Distinctive feature:** The Weighmaster's Station. Every shipment through ya-Don passes this checkpoint. The Covenant can choke supply lines simply by slowing processing.

---

### The Furnace Board

**What it does:** Coordinating body where capital (Aureate families), labor (guilds), the state (Bureau of the Rod), and the church (Arch-Curacy) negotiate production decisions.

**Cash flow:** No direct revenue—the Board is a negotiating forum. Administrative support ~50,000 obols/year.

**Decision-making:** Nine seats—three Aureate families (es-Kiral, es-Etaie, es-Vorath), four major guilds (Order, Unwritten-Axiom Parish, Covenant, Companions Chapter), Bureau liaison, and Arch-Curacy representative. Major decisions require six of nine votes.

The Aureates cannot outvote the guilds; the guilds cannot outvote Aureate-Bureau-Church alignment. This forces compromise.

**Distinctive feature:** Corporatism functioning as designed—competing interests forced to negotiate rather than fight.

---

### Unwritten-Axiom Parish

**What it does:** Elite craftsmen controlling deep-section Furnace production—lector components, weapons systems, technologies whose manufacturing processes are secret. Approximately 4,000 members including the five Verethani families (endogamous Highborn whose voices enable critical processes).

**Cash flow in:**
- Estimated production value: 3-5 million obols/year
- Parish receives perhaps 40% as wages and benefits: 1.2-2 million obols/year

**Cash flow out:**
- Member wages (higher than Order average): 400-600 obols/month for senior members
- Verethani family support
- Secret facilities maintenance
- Heterodox religious operations

**Decision-making:** The "Curate" is appointed by union leadership and functions as master artisan and guardian of secrets. The five Verethani family heads hold effective veto over production requiring their voices.

**Distinctive feature:** The Parish is a Gnostic tech-cult. Their ceremonies are manufacturing processes. Their theology treats the incomprehensibility of deep-section production as sacred mystery.
