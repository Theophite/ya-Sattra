# Horizon Row

> **Type:** Neighborhood (commercial street)
> **Location:** Tiers 450-572, on top of rotated arcology exterior
> **Function:** Commercial hub, infrastructure services
> **Character:** Almost feels normal—Guild-like propriety

Horizon Row occupies the arcology's exterior walls, which now form elevated ground. Buildings are constructed directly onto this wall surface, using the arcology's outer shell as foundation.

## Physical Character

Walking Horizon Row feels almost normal. The cobblestone street stretches clean and level, shopfronts displaying goods with Guild-like propriety. The air smells of stone dust and ceramic lamps rather than the harbor stink pervading the Iron Yards below.

If you don't look up at the angled skyline or notice the characteristic gait of locals, you might think you're in Medina.

**The reality beneath normalcy** becomes obvious when you enter the Arcades through the access points along the Row.

## Key Establishments

**Infrastructure:**
- **Val Waterworks** — Water distribution, public bathhouses with hot water
- **Three-Token Exchange** — Moneychanging house

**Services:**
- **Copper-Kettle Clinic** — Chemical exposure and radiation treatment
- **Clean-Hall Decontamination** — Salvage purity certification (Inspector Lasim Dray)
- **Thread-&-Spark Lector Works** — Reconditioned devices, run by Darim Loak (sewer-fisher family with Ridge presence)

**Commerce:**
- General stores, pharmacies
- Equipment suppliers for Arcade workshops

## Connections

**Outward:**
- **Rubble Road** to Medina Quarter (primary commercial connection, equal elevation)
- **Main Staircase** descending to Middens and Iron Yards (external, built on arcology surface)
- **Adit tunnels, cable lifts** to Iron Yards

**Inward:**
- Multiple access points into the Arcades
- Various corridor entrances to interior workshop district

## Population

Houses skilled craftspeople, shopkeepers, and journeymen from the Companions Guild and other technical organizations. These are people with:
- Established businesses
- Regular income
- Professional standing

Their homes are carefully converted spaces in buildings on the arcology wall—wedge-shaped furniture, leveled interiors showing long-term investment in making the district livable.
