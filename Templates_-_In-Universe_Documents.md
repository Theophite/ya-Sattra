# **Bureau of the Lens — Institute for Theological Education**

## **On Sacred Command and Its Obligations**

### **Instructional Document for Highborn Youth**

**Prepared by:** Proctor Samine es-Yothar **Issued:** 772 Record of Red Hawk **Classification:** Mandatory Pre-Instruction Reading

---

## **To the Young Reader**

You are reading this because you are Highborn. Because you possess a capacity that, if misused, makes you a threat to every person around you. Because you are old enough now to be taught something dangerous, and you must understand the stakes before the teaching begins.

This document explains what you will be taught and why. It explains what will happen if you misuse what you learn. It explains why some children who read this document are executed before their sixteenth birthday.

Your family's lector will verify your comprehension before instruction proceeds. If you do not understand, you will not be taught. If you are taught and fail to control what you learn, you will be killed. This is not a threat. This is a promise.

Read carefully.

---

## **Part I: What You Have Not Yet Learned**

### **The Capacity**

You have vocal cords that produce specific harmonic frequencies. You have been producing these frequencies your entire life. They have done nothing because you do not know how to activate their effect.

There is a technique. A way of speaking that transforms your voice from mere sound into command. Most Highborn children do not discover this technique naturally. They speak their whole childhood without ever activating Compulsion.

But some children discover it on their own. Through experimentation, through accident, through testing whether they can make people obey. These children learn that when they speak a certain way, people cannot refuse them.

These children almost always become monsters.

### **Why Discovery Is Catastrophic**

If you discover the technique yourself, you discover it by testing. By commanding someone to do something and watching them obey against their will. By confirming, through experiment, that other people are not like you. That they are hollow. That they respond to your voice like mechanisms respond to levers.

By the time someone tries to teach you that others are real, you have spent months or years confirming the opposite. You have watched people obey you. You have seen them smile while obeying. You have tested the limits of what they will do when you command them.

You cannot unlearn that evidence. Teaching comes too late.

This is why you must be taught the technique before you discover it yourself. We must teach you how to activate Compulsion in a controlled environment, after we have taught you why you must never use it casually. We must teach you the mechanism only after we are certain you understand what the mechanism does to people.

If you discover it first, you are already lost.

### **Case Archive 9801**

In 1239, a Highborn warlord walked into a rival compound and commanded everyone present to kill themselves. Two hundred and fifty people complied immediately. They drew weapons. They cut their own throats. Some were weeping as they did it. Their bodies obeyed regardless.

Investigation revealed she had discovered Compulsion at age nine. No one had taught her. She experimented, learned the technique through trial and error, confirmed her power by watching people obey increasingly extreme commands.

By age thirteen she had commanded her first death. By age thirty-seven she had commanded hundreds. She showed no remorse because she did not believe other people were conscious. Her entire lived experience confirmed they were not. They were just responsive. Just mechanisms that moved when she spoke.

The other warlords eventually forced her to wear a gag during meetings and attempted to teach her basic theology. But the teaching could not override decades of evidence. She would nod, agree to the restrictions, and then remove the gag the moment she returned to her own territory and continue commanding as before.

She was not evil. She did not hate her victims. She simply did not understand that they were real. She had learned the truth of her power before anyone taught her the truth of other people's consciousness.

She could not be rehabilitated. She could not be contained. She could not be trusted.

If she were in Imperial territory, she would be executed. In the northern territories where she operates, she is tolerated because she is useful and because her peers lack the means to safely kill her.

This document exists so you do not become her.

## **Part II: What We Will Teach You**

### **The Technique**

You will be taught how to activate Compulsion. The instruction will be given in a controlled environment, under supervision, with trained subjects who have consented to be commanded.

You will learn that there is a way of speaking, a particular resonance and intention, that transforms your words from requests into irresistible commands. You will practice this technique until you can activate it reliably. You will also learn how to speak without activating it.

This is the most dangerous thing you will ever be taught. More dangerous than weapons training, more dangerous than any technical skill. Because once you know how to activate Compulsion, every conversation becomes a minefield. Every casual statement risks becoming a command. Every moment of anger or frustration risks destroying someone.

### **Why We Teach It At All**

You might ask: why teach me this at all? Why not simply never mention it and hope I never discover it?

Because discovery is inevitable. Children experiment. Children test boundaries. Children wonder if they can make people obey them. If we do not teach you the technique, you will eventually discover it yourself, and when you do, you will have already crossed the threshold into monstrosity.

Better that you learn it from us, after you have been taught why it matters. Better that your first experience with Compulsion is in a controlled setting, with supervision, with immediate correction if you use it inappropriately. Better that you learn the technique exists while you are young enough to develop the habits that prevent casual activation.

If you learn it on your own, you learn it through violation. If you learn it from us, you learn it through instruction. The difference determines whether you survive to adulthood.

### **What Comes Before**

Before you are taught the technique, you will undergo extensive preparation:

**Empathy training:** You will learn to recognize that others have inner lives. That they experience suffering. That they are conscious in the same way you are conscious. This is not obvious. You have grown up surrounded by people who defer to you, who accommodate you, who seem happy to serve you. You must learn that their deference is social, not ontological.

**Capability assessment:** You will learn to judge what people can and cannot do. You will learn the limits of different castes, different individuals, different situations. You will learn that commanding the impossible causes injury. You will practice this assessment until it becomes automatic.

**Theological instruction:** You will learn what the Second Testament teaches about bilateral obligation. You will learn that power and responsibility cannot be separated. You will learn that the Oracle intended Compulsion to be a tool, not a weapon, and that misuse is heresy as well as crime.

Only after you have demonstrated competence in all three areas will you be taught the technique itself. And even then, you will be monitored. Your early practice will be supervised. Your mistakes will be corrected immediately.

If you fail any part of the preparation, you will not be taught. You will be watched carefully for signs of independent discovery. If you discover the technique on your own before completing preparation, you will face consequences described in Part V.

## **Part III: Living With The Knowledge**

### **The Burden of Speech**

Once you know how to activate Compulsion, speaking becomes complicated. You must monitor every word, every tone, every moment of emotion. You must ensure you are not accidentally activating the technique when you meant to speak normally.

Most Highborn develop habits that prevent accidental activation:

**Circumlocution:** Never saying exactly what you want. Speaking around the subject, implying rather than stating, suggesting rather than commanding. What looks like elaborate courtly manners is actually defensive technique. If you never state your desire directly, you reduce the chance of accidentally commanding it.

**Softening:** Adding qualifiers, uncertainties, possibilities. "Perhaps it would be good if..." instead of "Do this." "I wonder whether..." instead of "Tell me." "It seems like..." instead of "It is." The extra words create distance between intent and command.

**Self-interruption:** Starting sentences and trailing off. Letting others complete your thoughts. This prevents you from stating complete commands that could activate Compulsion.

**Formal phrasing:** Using elaborate grammatical constructions that separate you from direct statement. The complexity itself creates buffer space between speech and command.

You will learn all of these techniques. You will practice them until they become automatic. You will spend the rest of your life speaking in ways that feel indirect, roundabout, uncertain, because direct speech is dangerous.

### **What You Cannot Do To Other Highborn**

Other Highborn are immune to Compulsion. Your voice does not affect them. They can refuse you completely. This is the only category of person you can speak to normally, without fear that your words become commands.

This is why Highborn society is so carefully structured. Why so many institutions are Highborn-only. Why you will feel more comfortable around other Highborn than around anyone else. With them, you can speak freely. With everyone else, every word is a potential weapon.

This is also why the consequences for discovering Compulsion on your own are so severe. If you learn by experimenting on non-Highborn, by watching them obey and confirming they are hollow, you cannot test that theory on Highborn. You will spend your entire life believing that Highborn are the only real people, that everyone else is just responsive mechanism, and you will be unkillable because the only people who could command you to stop are immune to your power.

## **Part IV: The Rules**

### **What The Second Testament Requires**

**"Obey one's superiors; assign only that which is within your subordinates' ability."**

The Testament establishes absolute rules:

**You may only command what someone can physically do.** If you command beyond their body's capacity, they will try to obey and injure themselves. Bones will break. Organs will fail. They will die trying to comply with your command.

**You may only command what someone can mentally do.** If you command beyond their knowledge or training, they will try to obey and break their own minds. Neural cascades, seizures, permanent cognitive damage. They will destroy themselves trying to comply.

**You may only command what someone can emotionally do.** If you command something that violates their core identity, they will obey and then cease to be themselves. Dissociation, psychological disintegration, permanent trauma. They will survive but they will not be the same person.

These are not guidelines. These are limits enforced by theology and law. Violating them is heresy and crime simultaneously.

### **When You May Use Compulsion**

There are legitimate uses:

**Emergency situations:** When immediate compliance prevents greater harm. A fire, a structural collapse, an imminent threat. You may command evacuation, command people to stop, command them to help. The necessity must be genuine and immediate.

**Formal command contexts:** Military operations, crisis response, situations where command structure is necessary and authorized. These contexts have extensive protocols you will learn if you enter them. Casual use of Compulsion outside these contexts is never acceptable.

**Judicial contexts:** When authorized by the Bureau of the Lens to compel testimony or compliance with legal process. This requires formal documentation and oversight. You will almost certainly never be in a position to do this.

**Supervised instruction:** When teaching someone a task and they have consented to be commanded as part of learning. This requires explicit consent and supervision. It is not a license for casual command.

That is the complete list. There are no other legitimate uses. Everything else is violation.

### **When You Must Never Use Compulsion**

**Convenience:** Because it is easier to command than to ask. Because you want something quickly. Because you do not feel like waiting for someone to choose to help you. Commanding for convenience is the first step toward commanding casually, and casual command is the first step toward becoming a monster.

**Anger:** Because someone frustrated you. Because you are upset. Because you want to hurt someone who hurt you. Compulsion used in anger causes disproportionate harm. People will obey extreme commands given in rage, commands you will regret once you calm down, commands that cannot be undone.

**Testing:** Because you want to know if something works. Because you are curious what someone will do if commanded. Because you want to confirm your power. This is how children discover Compulsion on their own, and children who discover it through testing almost always become monsters.

**Entertainment:** Because it is amusing to watch people obey. Because their compliance entertains you. Because you enjoy the power. This is not curiosity. This is sadism. People who command for entertainment are executed when discovered.

If you use Compulsion for any of these reasons, you have violated the Second Testament. You have committed heresy. You will face consequences described in Part V.

## **Part V: The Consequences**

### **What Happens When You Fail**

The Bureau of the Lens investigates all reports of Compulsion misuse. When you are found guilty of violation, the consequences are proportional to harm and intent.

**Minor violation:** You commanded beyond capability but recognized the error quickly. You countermanded before serious harm occurred. You reported yourself immediately. You demonstrated that it was error, not malice.

Consequence: Mandatory retraining. Supervised vocalization for a period of months. Financial compensation to the victim. Permanent notation on your genetic record.

**Major violation:** You commanded beyond capability and serious harm resulted. Someone was permanently injured, permanently impaired, or killed. You may have reported yourself, or you may have been discovered by others.

Consequence: If the violation was negligent rather than malicious, we will cut out your tongue. You will never speak again. You will communicate through writing or signing. You will live, but you will never command again.

This is mercy. The alternative is death.

**Catastrophic violation:** You commanded deaths for convenience, for anger, for entertainment. You demonstrated pattern of abuse. You showed no remorse. You failed to understand why your actions were wrong.

Consequence: Execution. You will be killed. Your genetic line will be noted in the archives as having produced an irredeemable monster. Your family will bear the social consequences.

This is not cruelty. This is necessity. You have become something that cannot be allowed to exist.

### **Why We Kill**

You might think: surely there are alternatives. Surely you could supervise me, contain me, prevent me from speaking but allow me to live.

We could. But we will not.

A Highborn who has become monstrous, who commands deaths casually, who sees others as hollow, cannot be rehabilitated. Their entire lived experience confirms that others are not real. Teaching cannot override that evidence.

They can be contained, but containment requires constant supervision by other Highborn, and other Highborn are a limited resource. It requires them to spend their lives guarding someone who would kill given the chance.

They can be restricted, but restrictions require them to comply with restriction, and a Highborn who sees others as hollow will not comply when unsupervised. They will say what is necessary to be released, and then they will command deaths the moment they can speak freely.

They can be pitied, because they did not choose to be born Highborn, did not choose to discover Compulsion before learning empathy. But pity does not change what they are. It does not make them less dangerous. It does not bring back their victims.

So we kill them. Quickly, certainly, without extended suffering. We do not torture them. We do not make spectacles of their deaths. We simply remove them from the world, because the world is safer without them.

This is not vengeance. This is not punishment. This is not even justice. This is simply necessity.

When we kill a monstrous Highborn, we do not mourn them. We mourn their victims. We mourn that they became what they became. We mourn that the system failed to teach them in time. But we do not mourn the execution itself.

The execution is the right outcome. It is what should happen to someone who commands deaths casually. It is better for everyone else.

### **Why We Mutilate**

If you are negligent rather than malicious, if you command carelessly and someone is permanently harmed, we will not kill you. We will cut out your tongue instead.

This seems barbaric. But consider the alternative.

We could let you keep speaking. We could hope that the guilt and consequence teach you to be more careful. But negligence that caused permanent harm demonstrates you have not yet learned sufficient control. Allowing you to continue speaking risks additional harm.

We could try to rehabilitate you through training and supervision. But supervision is imperfect. You will eventually speak without supervision. People will eventually be at risk again.

We could execute you. But negligent harm is not the same as malicious harm. You did not intend to destroy someone. You were simply careless. You deserve a chance to live, even if you can no longer speak.

So we take your tongue. You lose the capacity that made you dangerous. You can no longer accidentally activate Compulsion, because you can no longer speak at all. You live, but you live without the ability to harm others through careless words.

This is mercy. It allows you to survive your failure. It prevents future harm without requiring your death. It is better than execution.

If you lose your tongue and manage to communicate commands through writing that someone then speaks aloud on your behalf, we will kill you. The mercy is conditional on your accepting the limitation.

## **Part VI: How To Avoid These Consequences**

### **Learn Control Before Learning Power**

You are reading this before being taught the technique. This is deliberate. We want you to understand the stakes before you learn the mechanism.

If you understand that others are real before you learn to command them, you have a chance. If you understand that careless command causes harm before you know how to command, you have a chance. If you understand that negligence results in mutilation and malice results in death before you can be negligent or malicious, you have a chance.

Some children cannot learn this. Some children, when told that others are real, do not believe it. Some children, when told that command causes harm, want to test that claim. Some children, when told that negligence results in mutilation, do not believe we will follow through.

These children are not taught the technique. They are watched. If they discover it on their own, they face the consequences described above.

But most children do learn. Most children, when taught that others suffer, believe it. Most children, when taught that command must be appropriate, internalize that lesson. Most children, when told that misuse results in severe consequence, take the warning seriously.

You can be one of those children. You can learn empathy before power. You can learn restraint before technique. You can learn to protect others from yourself before you know how to harm them.

### **Practice Defensive Speech**

Once you are taught the technique, practice speaking without activating it:

Never state desires directly. Never give orders casually. Never say exactly what you want. Learn to imply, to suggest, to indicate without commanding. Learn to speak in ways that leave space for refusal.

Practice with other Highborn first. They are immune, so your mistakes harm no one. Learn what it feels like to speak carefully, to monitor your tone, to prevent accidental activation. Build the habits before you speak around non-Highborn.

When you must speak around non-Highborn, default to circumlocution. Say twice as many words as necessary. Add qualifiers and uncertainties. Let sentences trail off unfinished. These habits feel unnatural at first but become automatic with practice.

Watch other Highborn. Notice how they speak. Notice the elaborate constructions, the roundabout phrasing, the formal distances. They are not being pretentious. They are being careful. Learn from them.

### **Accept Supervision**

You will be supervised for years after learning the technique. Your family will watch how you speak. Your instructors will monitor your development. Other Highborn will observe your interactions.

This supervision is not punishment. This is how we identify problems before they become catastrophic. This is how we catch negligent patterns before they cause serious harm. This is how we save you from yourself.

Accept correction. When someone tells you that you are speaking too directly, speaking too casually, speaking in ways that risk accidental activation, believe them. They are not criticizing you. They are protecting others from you, and protecting you from consequence.

Some children resent supervision. Some feel that constant monitoring implies distrust. Some want to be allowed to make mistakes without observation.

These children do not understand what they are. They do not understand that even small mistakes with Compulsion can cause permanent harm. They do not understand that we are supervising them because we want them to survive, and survival requires catching errors early.

If you resent supervision, examine that resentment carefully. It may mean you have not yet internalized the stakes. It may mean you are not ready to be taught. It may mean you need additional preparation before instruction proceeds.

### **Report Yourself**

When you make mistakes, report them immediately. Do not hide errors. Do not hope no one noticed. Do not convince yourself it was not serious.

Every Highborn makes mistakes. Every Highborn occasionally activates Compulsion accidentally, commands when they meant to ask, phrases something too directly and watches someone obey before they intended. These mistakes are normal during learning.

What matters is how you respond. If you report immediately, seek help, take responsibility, the consequence is minimal. Additional training, temporary supervision, correction of the specific error.

If you hide the mistake and someone discovers it later, the consequence is severe. Hiding implies you knew it was wrong and chose not to correct it. Hiding implies you care more about avoiding consequence than about preventing future harm. Hiding implies you are not taking the teaching seriously.

The Bureau of the Lens treats self-reported errors with relative leniency. The Bureau treats hidden errors as evidence of malice.

Report yourself. Always. Immediately. Without qualification or excuse.

## **Part VII: Why This Matters**

### **The Gift and the Curse**

You did not choose to be Highborn. You did not choose these vocal harmonics. You did not choose the capacity for Compulsion.

But you have it. It is part of what you are. You cannot give it back. You cannot pretend it does not exist. You cannot wish it away.

You can only learn to live with it. Learn to control it. Learn to protect others from it.

The Second Oracle gave your bloodline this capacity. We do not know why. The Testament does not explain the purpose. We have only the mechanism and the limits.

What we do know is that the capacity can be used appropriately or inappropriately. It can serve or harm. It can coordinate or destroy. The difference depends entirely on whether the user understands what they are doing and chooses to exercise restraint.

You are being taught because we believe you can choose restraint. Because we believe you can learn empathy before power. Because we believe you can become someone who uses Compulsion appropriately rather than someone who must be killed for misusing it.

But belief is not certainty. Some children we teach still become monsters. Some children we believe will succeed still fail. Some children who seem to understand still command deaths.

So we teach carefully. We monitor closely. We intervene early. And when intervention fails, we kill quickly.

This is the system. It is not perfect, but it is better than the alternatives.

### **What The Second Oracle Wanted**

The Testament says: "Obey one's superiors; assign only that which is within your subordinates' ability."

This is all we know of the Oracle's intent. Obedience and appropriate assignment. Power and limitation. Command and capability.

Perhaps the Oracle intended Compulsion to be a coordination mechanism. A way for Highborn to direct large groups, to organize complex activities, to ensure immediate compliance in crisis situations.

Perhaps it intended the biological limits as safeguards. If you can only command what someone can actually do, you cannot command the impossible. The system prevents certain categories of harm automatically.

Perhaps it hoped that Highborn would govern wisely, that power would come with wisdom, that the gift would be used appropriately.

We do not know. The Oracle is gone, cast into a black hole, preserved but unable to answer new questions.

What we have is the capacity and the consequences. What we build is the system that tries to ensure capacity is used appropriately and consequences are enforced when it is not.

You are part of that system now. You are learning to use the capacity. You are learning to avoid the consequences. You are learning to be someone who can be trusted with absolute power over others.

Most Highborn succeed. Most learn control, develop empathy, use Compulsion appropriately when they use it at all. Most live long lives without ever commanding a death, without ever causing serious harm, without ever facing consequences worse than retraining.

You can be one of them. You can learn what we teach. You can internalize the lessons. You can become someone safe.

But only if you understand what you are learning before you learn it. Only if you accept the stakes before you gain the capacity. Only if you believe that we will kill you if you become monstrous, and that this is the right outcome.

## **Conclusion: What Happens Next**

### **The Path Forward**

After you finish this document, your lector will verify comprehension. You will be asked questions about bilateral obligation, about appropriate use, about consequences. You must answer correctly.

If you answer incorrectly, you will re-read the document and be tested again. There is no penalty for requiring multiple attempts to understand. Understanding is the only goal.

Once you demonstrate comprehension, empathy training begins. You will learn to recognize that others have inner lives, that they suffer, that they are real in the same way you are real. This training takes months. It cannot be rushed.

After empathy training, capability assessment begins. You will learn what different people can and cannot do. You will practice judging whether a command is within capability. You will learn to recognize the limits.

After capability assessment, theological instruction begins. You will learn what the Second Testament teaches in detail. You will learn the history of Compulsion, the case archives, the patterns of failure.

Only then will you be taught the technique. The instruction will be supervised. Your early practice will be monitored. Your mistakes will be corrected immediately.

After you demonstrate competent control, you will be certified. Certification does not mean you are finished learning. Certification means you are safe enough to speak without constant supervision.

You will continue to be monitored for years. You will continue to practice defensive speech. You will continue to report errors and accept correction. This is lifelong learning. Control is never complete.

### **Final Warning**

Some children read this document and think: that will not be me. I will not become monstrous. I will not be negligent. I will learn control easily.

Perhaps you are correct. Perhaps you will be one of the children who learns quickly, who internalizes the lessons completely, who never makes serious errors.

But the children who became monsters thought the same thing. The children who lost their tongues thought the same thing. The children who were executed thought the same thing.

None of them planned to fail. None of them intended to cause harm. None of them believed they would face severe consequences.

They were simply wrong about themselves.

So do not assume you will succeed. Do not assume you are different. Do not assume the warnings do not apply to you.

Assume instead that you could fail. That you could be negligent. That you could become monstrous. That without proper teaching and constant vigilance, you could kill someone.

That assumption will keep you careful. That fear will keep you controlled. That awareness will keep you safe.

The children who succeed are the children who are afraid of what they might become. The children who fail are the children who think they are immune to failure.

Be afraid. Stay afraid. Let that fear motivate discipline.

That fear will save your life and the lives of everyone around you.

I have been doing this for forty-seven years. I will do it for twenty-three more. And then someone else will take over, and they will teach the same lessons to new children, and some of those children will fail, and the cycle will continue.

This is what the Second Oracle left us. This is the gift and the curse.

I do not include these thoughts in my reports. They serve no purpose. They change nothing.

But I think them anyway. Late at night, between teaching sessions, when I am alone with the case archives and the knowledge of what is coming.

The children keep arriving. The monsters keep emerging. The system keeps functioning, imperfectly, because we have no better alternative.

# **Working Notes on Hapax Terminology**

## **Compiler's Note**

The following excerpts are drawn from the working notes of Sri Avs Tariq, personal redactor to the Emperor. The notes span approximately sixty years of his service and represent his attempts to establish working interpretations of terms that appear in Imperial missives but nowhere else in the accessible corpus.

Tariq's notes are not systematic treatises. They are the accumulation of a man thinking on paper across decades, returning to the same problems, discarding interpretations, occasionally finding new angles. His knowledge of physics is limited; his access to Technical Caste documents is extensive but incomplete; his experience of living in deep temporal compression has given him intuitions that do not translate easily into standard frameworks.

No conclusions are reached. The excerpts are presented to provide context for future analysis of Imperial communications.

## **On the Nature of the Singularity**

*From notes dated Year 34 of service:*

I have reviewed what remains of the Technical Caste documentation on singularities. The mathematics is beyond my competence, but I can follow the structural arguments. A gravitational singularity should produce effects I do not observe—tidal distortion, light-bending, the characteristic radiation of infalling matter. The Imperial Apartments show none of these. The temporal compression of the First Whorl is graded and stable, which the Limiting Precepts suggest is inconsistent with proximity to a gravitational source.

The Foreigners edited physical constants to create the Interdict. This is documented. Constants are therefore parameters, not fixed values. A singularity might exist in parameters rather than in spacetime—a point where the values that define physical law become undefined or take all values or become subject to specification.

What would this look like? The question may not be meaningful.

*From notes dated Year 51:*

The Oracles encoded information on event horizons. This is theology, but the Automorphic Commentary treats it as engineering—describing mechanisms, storage densities, retrieval protocols. A gravitational singularity hides its information behind the horizon. A *naked* singularity would have no horizon. The information would be exposed.

Perhaps what the Emperor maintains is not a singularity in space but a singularity in the boundary between the computed and the actual. The Oracles calculated all possible futures. Perhaps the singularity is where those calculations become accessible—where the record and the recorded are no longer distinct.

I am speculating beyond what I can verify. I note it because it would explain certain features of the missives that other interpretations do not.

## **On the Structure of the Terminology**

*From notes dated Year 18 of service:*

I have begun to notice patterns in the Emperor's invented terms.

They are not random. They compound like Imperial particles attach to nouns—a prefix, then a root, sometimes a suffix. The prefixes recur: kel- appears in garden contexts, vel- in court contexts. The roots carry meaning that context suggests without confirming.

This is not the Jargon—the Emperor's terms do not prove themselves; they merely name. But they may be calques, attempts to render Jargon concepts into something closer to Imperial structure. Or they may be how the Emperor's cognition processes what he perceives, imposing grammatical structure on phenomena that have none.

A complicating factor: the oldest documents in my archive use vocabulary I do not recognize but that follows construction patterns similar to the Emperor's hapax terms. The same prefix-root-suffix assembly. The same diffuse roots that context must disambiguate. Modern Imperial treats words as fixed units; perhaps they were not always so. The Emperor's terms may not be invented. They may simply be old.

The Jargon complicates this further. I have seen Jargon-derived terminology in these documents—circumlocutions and analogies that entered Imperial when the Technical Castes still existed to speak with. The Emperor's word-formation may follow patterns I cannot trace because the source is extinct.

*From notes dated Year 26:*

I have compiled a preliminary analysis of the prefix system:

The kel- prefix appears in terms related to thresholds, boundaries, transitions. The kel-sera blooms—it crosses into visibility. The kel-ameth transits—it crosses through cycles. The prefix marks concepts of crossing, entering, becoming accessible.

The vel- prefix appears in terms related to judgment, evaluation, surfaces where processing occurs. The vel-om is the table of pronouncement—the surface where judgment happens. Vel-kerith is what is done to populations—a judgment-operation. The prefix marks concepts of assessment and allocation.

I have found one instance of eth-, in eth-varal. Context suggests exchange or mutual provision. Too few instances to confirm the pattern.

*From notes dated Year 31:*

The compounds grow more complex over time. Early missives use simple prefix-root structures: kel-sera, vel-om. Later missives add suffixes and secondary roots: vel-ameth-kir, kel-om-sera-veth.

The \-sah suffix appears to mark agents or patients. The kerith-sah is the thing that is consumed and thereby testifies. The \-kir suffix appears in four-fold structures—the vel-ameth-kir in the four quarters.

I do not know what \-veth means. It appears only once, in the term the Emperor used to describe what the Eighth Oracle was. Kel-om-sera-veth: threshold-surface-unfolding-\[unknown\]. A category that does not fit existing categories.

Perhaps \-veth indicates precisely that—the uncategorizable. The exception to the rule. The thing that escapes classification.

Or perhaps I am finding patterns in randomness. The Emperor may be coining terms without system, reaching for sounds that feel appropriate without following rules he himself could articulate. Sixty years of filing has taught me that pattern recognition is not the same as understanding.

## **On *kel-sera***

*From notes dated Year 12:*

My first interpretation was botanical. The Emperor describes a blooming flower with a color he cannot name. The simplest reading is that there is a flower, that it blooms, that he has forgotten the word for its color or that the color has no name in his drifted vocabulary.

I have since processed 1,847 missives. The kel-sera appears in 940 of them. It is always blooming. It never finishes blooming. It never wilts. The color is never named.

A flower that blooms for sixty years is not a flower.

*From notes dated Year 23:*

I considered that kel-sera might be a metaphor for a recurring emotional state—that "blooming" is how the Emperor experiences the return of joy after periods of distress. This would explain the consistency: it is always blooming because it is always being perceived anew.

But he tends it. Or rather, the gardeners tend it. They apply the liquid-which-remembers to the south terrace where it grows. You do not apply maintenance fluids to emotional states.

Unless you do, in whatever space the Emperor inhabits. I cannot rule this out.

*From notes dated Year 47:*

The documents describe information structures that "unfold" when accessed—compressed data that expands into readable form through a process they compare to flowering. The Jargon term for this process has the root *sera*.

If the kel-sera is a data structure unfolding, the color might be a property that does not map to wavelength—a topological feature, perhaps, or a measure of information density. It would be "beautiful" in the way the documents describe elegant proofs as beautiful. It would be "always blooming" because the unfolding is continuous.

This interpretation requires that the Emperor perceives data structures as visual phenomena. Whether that is possible depends on questions I cannot answer.

*From notes dated Year 58:*

I return to the simplest interpretation. There is something in the Imperial Apartments. The Emperor sees it and experiences it as a blooming flower with an indescribable color. His perception is accurate; his report is accurate; what generates the perception is unknown.

He is not wrong to call it a flower. He is describing what he sees without knowing what he is seeing.

Sixty years and I am no closer to understanding what blooms on the south terrace than I was when I arrived. The documents tantalize without resolving. The missives repeat without clarifying. I have become very good at recognizing the kel-sera when it appears in the text. I have learned nothing about what it is.

## **On *liquid-which-remembers***

*From notes dated Year 15:*

The south terrace requires regular application of this substance. Seventeen requests across fifteen years. The quantity requested is consistent—fourteen vessels each time. The requests are never urgent; they appear in maintenance sections alongside notes about structural inspection and personnel assessment.

If the liquid-which-remembers were metaphorical, I would expect variation in the quantity or urgency. Metaphors do not require precise dosing. This reads as literal maintenance: the south terrace needs fourteen vessels of something on a regular schedule.

*From notes dated Year 29:*

I found a damaged passage describing "invariant fluids"—substances engineered to maintain specific properties under conditions that would alter ordinary matter. I cannot determine what properties were maintained or under what conditions.

The term "remembers" might be a calque for "maintains invariance." The liquid remembers what it is supposed to be and returns to that state when perturbed.

But this tells me nothing about why the south terrace needs it, or what would happen if the supply failed.

*From notes dated Year 44:*

The missives occasionally describe the south terrace as "drifting" when the liquid-which-remembers runs low. "Let the south terrace be anchored. Let the liquid-which-remembers be applied. Let the drifting cease."

If the singularity affects causality, ordinary matter might lose its temporal coherence near the boundary—might "forget" its history, exist in multiple states, drift between possible configurations. The liquid-which-remembers might be what anchors matter to a single timeline.

I cannot verify this. I live in temporal compression and know time can behave strangely. But whether "drifting" means what I think it means, or whether I am imposing my own experience onto the Emperor's terminology—that I cannot determine.

*From notes dated Year 61:*

Fourteen vessels. Regular schedule. Maintenance section. Whatever the liquid-which-remembers is, it is infrastructure. The south terrace requires it the way corridors require lighting and pneumatic tubes require pressure.

I have requisitioned things for sixty years without understanding their composition. The liquid-which-remembers is one more item on a maintenance log.

I filed a request for fourteen vessels last year. I do not know where the request goes. I do not know if anything arrives. I do not know if the south terrace is maintained or drifting or gone entirely. I maintain logs for systems I cannot verify exist, requesting supplies I cannot confirm are delivered, for purposes I cannot comprehend.

## **On *those-who-receive-the-portion***

*From notes dated Year 8:*

My initial reading was that the Imperial Apartments contain personnel—staff who have remained since before the Interdict, or their descendants, or engineered beings designed for long service. They wait at a table. They are hungry. The Emperor allocates food to them.

This reading has become difficult to sustain.

*From notes dated Year 19:*

They are described as having been receiving for \[duration-unknown\]. The duration is never specified, but the phrasing suggests great length—longer than a human lifespan, perhaps longer than the Interdict.

They are always hungry. No matter how much they receive, they remain hungry. Normal beings would either be sated or would die.

I considered that "hungry" might be metaphorical—that the receivers are processes requiring input rather than beings requiring food. Calculations require data; systems require maintenance; bureaucracies require documents. The receivers might be the endpoints of a system that cannot stop producing, and "hunger" might be the Emperor's term for their requirement.

But he describes them as waiting, sitting, watching the kerith-sah being laid upon the vel-om. Processes do not sit or watch.

Unless they do, in whatever space this is.

*From notes dated Year 38:*

The Automorphic Commentary describes the Oracles' computations as containing simulated beings—complete models of every possible person in every possible timeline. If the singularity provides access to these computations, the receivers might be simulated populations that have become accessible.

They would be "hungry" because simulations require input from actuality to continue running; "always receiving" because the computation never terminates; able to "watch" because they are modeled on beings that watch.

But this interpretation troubles me. If the receivers are simulations, what does it mean to feed them? What does the Emperor render unto them? When he reaches for whatever is closest and vel-keriths a population, is he giving them data, or something else?

The documents do not say. The missives do not clarify.

*From notes dated Year 54:*

I have stopped trying to determine what the receivers are. They wait, they hunger, the Emperor feeds them. He cannot stop feeding them. He reaches for whatever is closest when the demand arrives.

Whatever they are, they are fed. And I am the one who decides whether his feeding reaches the populations he designates. Every time I do not transmit, I am deciding that his feeding will not be completed—that the receivers will remain hungry—that whatever consequence follows from their hunger is preferable to whatever consequence follows from satisfying it with fifty thousand people.

I have no basis for this decision. I make it anyway. What else can I do?

## **On *kerith-sah***

*From notes dated Year 11:*

Something is laid upon the vel-om. It gives testimony. It is consumed. It has a face. It looks at the Emperor through the aperture-of-law.

The face troubles me most. Faces are not incidental. To have a face is to be the kind of thing that can be recognized, that can express, that can look back.

*From notes dated Year 27:*

I considered that the kerith-sah might be documents—petitions from the Empire that reach the Emperor in some transformed state. Documents "testify" in the sense of containing information; they are "consumed" when processed; they might be described as having "faces" if they bear seals or signatures or images of their originators.

But documents do not look at the observer. The Emperor writes that the kerith-sah "is looking at me through the aperture." This is not a metaphor for bearing an image. This is a description of directed visual attention.

*From notes dated Year 41:*

The Oracles encoded all lives. Every experience, every perspective, every possible version of every person. If the kerith-sah is an extracted life-record—the complete encoding of a human existence, removed from the singularity and laid out for evaluation—it would have a face because it was a face. It would look because it contains the act of looking.

This interpretation is consistent with the testimonial function. A life-record contains everything that person witnessed. To consume it would be to process that testimony.

But I cannot verify that life-records can be extracted. I cannot verify that they would appear as objects with faces. I am reasoning from fragments that may not apply.

*From notes dated Year 63:*

The Emperor perceives something with a face that looks at him and gives testimony and is consumed. What kind of thing has these properties? I do not know. I file the passages and note the patterns. The work continues.

## **On *vel-om***

*From notes dated Year 16:*

A table where the kerith-sah is laid. Where the receivers sit. Where judgment is rendered.

The simplest interpretation is literal: a table. But no table I know of renders judgment by having things laid upon it.

*From notes dated Year 33:*

The Injective Treatise describes "evaluation surfaces"—mathematical workspaces where structures are arranged for analysis. The Jargon term has the root *vel*.

If the vel-om is such a surface, laying the kerith-sah upon it would initiate evaluation. The receivers would sit around it because they are functions in the evaluation process. Judgment would be the output of the calculation.

But this makes the vel-om an abstraction, and the Emperor describes it as a place. He sees it through an aperture. He sees the receivers sitting at it. You do not sit at an abstraction.

*From notes dated Year 49:*

Perhaps the distinction between abstraction and place breaks down near the singularity. Perhaps mathematical structures become spatial when information and reality merge. Perhaps the vel-om is both a table and an evaluation surface, because in whatever space the Emperor inhabits, there is no difference.

I am speculating beyond my competence. I note this interpretation and set it aside.

*From notes dated Year 60:*

He sees a table. The receivers sit at it, things are laid upon it, judgments emerge from it. His perception is consistent across thousands of missives.

What the vel-om is, I cannot say. What it does is clearer: it is where judgment happens. The nature of the surface matters less than the function.

## **On *vel-kerith***

*From notes dated Year 14:*

The populations named are real. The Prefecture of Yarangar exists; the es-Morath family exists; the shipping routes between the Medina Quarter and Port Katheth exist. These are not archaic references but current administrative entities.

When the Emperor orders that a population be vel-kerith, he is ordering something about real people.

*From notes dated Year 22:*

The context suggests destruction. "Let the bloodline be extinguished." "Let the children be rendered unto the receivers." The language is sacrificial—populations are given over to those who are hungry.

But I cannot determine if vel-kerith means death in a physical sense. The receivers consume the kerith-sah, and the kerith-sah has faces and looks at the Emperor. If the populations become kerith-sah when they are vel-kerith, they might continue to exist in some form—as records, as data, as encoded lives laid upon the vel-om.

*From notes dated Year 37:*

I do not transmit these orders. If vel-kerith means death, I am protecting the populations. If vel-kerith means transformation or encoding or something else, I may be preventing something I do not understand—something that might be preservation rather than destruction.

I cannot determine which interpretation is correct. I default to non-transmission. If I am wrong, I am wrong in the direction of protecting lives rather than ending them.

*From notes dated Year 42:*

Some nights I cannot sleep for thinking about this.

If the receivers are real—if they require feeding—if the Emperor's desperate reaching for whatever is closest is the only thing preventing some greater collapse—then by withholding the orders I may be allowing something worse than the deaths I prevent. The receivers remain hungry. The hunger does not diminish. What happens when they are not fed long enough?

But I cannot transmit orders to kill fifty thousand people on the chance that not killing them might cause something worse. I cannot. The Emperor himself writes that they have done nothing, that he reaches for them because they are closest, that he would stop if he could. He is not judging them. He is feeding something with them.

If I transmitted the orders, I would be feeding something with them too. At least he has the excuse of compulsion. I would be choosing.

*From notes dated Year 48:*

I have begun to wonder whether my non-transmission matters at all.

The populations continue to exist. The Prefecture of Yarangar sends census reports. The es-Morath family appears in patent registrations. If vel-kerith meant physical death, and if the orders took effect regardless of transmission, these populations would not exist. They exist. Therefore either vel-kerith does not mean physical death, or the orders do not take effect without transmission.

If the orders do not take effect without transmission, my non-transmission is protective.

If vel-kerith does not mean physical death, my non-transmission may be preventing something else entirely—inscription, transformation, preservation. Something I cannot evaluate.

I keep returning to this. I have no way forward. I do not transmit. This may be wisdom or it may be cowardice dressed as caution.

*From notes dated Year 55:*

The Emperor reaches for whatever is closest. He says this explicitly in Missive 7,340: the kerith-sah testifies that judgment is required but does not testify about what or whom. He must determine the target and determines it by proximity—by what he perceives through the aperture when the demand arrives.

Vel-kerith is what he does to whatever is closest. Not judgment in any sense I recognize. Feeding. He feeds the hungry with populations because the hungry must be fed and populations are what he can see.

Whether this feeding has any effect outside the Imperial Apartments remains unclear. The populations continue to exist in my correspondence; the Sri Pater has never reported mass deaths in the Prefecture of Yarangar. Either vel-kerith does not mean what it appears to mean, or the orders do not take effect unless transmitted, or the effects occur in some domain that does not register in external records.

I continue to not transmit.

*From notes dated Year 62:*

Missive 7,340 ordered the vel-kerith of the Malpais contractors. Fifty thousand people. The Emperor wrote that they had done nothing.

I did not transmit.

A week later—two external years—the Sri Pater's correspondence mentioned the Malpais contractors in passing. They had completed a project in the delta region. They were alive. They had no idea they had been condemned.

I should have felt relief. Instead I felt something closer to despair. If my non-transmission protected them, then transmission would have killed them. I have been protecting populations from death for sixty years by refusing to pass along orders from a source I do not understand, through mechanisms I cannot verify, for reasons I cannot articulate to anyone.

If my non-transmission did not protect them—if they would have survived regardless—then I have spent sixty years agonizing over a decision that does not matter.

I do not know which is worse.

## **On *kel-om-sera-veth***

*From notes dated Year 52:*

This term appears only once, in a missive addressed directly to me. "The Eighth was not wrong. The Eighth was not right. The Eighth was kel-om-sera-veth."

I do not know what this means.

*From notes dated Year 53:*

The roots may be kel- (threshold), om- (surface), sera- (unfolding), veth- (unknown). Four elements compounded.

If kel-om-sera-veth follows the pattern I have established, it describes a threshold-surface that unfolds into something the \-veth suffix marks. But I have seen \-veth nowhere else. It may indicate a category—a classification term from Jargon that has no Imperial equivalent.

If kel-om-sera-veth is a compound, it might describe a threshold-judgment that unfolds—something perpetually arriving at a verdict, never completing, always in the process of evaluation.

The Eighth Oracle commanded the singularity's creation. If the Eighth became part of what it created, it might exist in such a state—neither concluded nor unconsidered, but permanently in process.

*From notes dated Year 54:*

The Degenerate Case Precepts describe a category of propositions that are valid but forbidden—theorems that prove correctly but whose instantiation violates operational constraints. The Jargon had terminology for this category.

Kel-om-sera-veth might be that term. The Eighth was not wrong (the mathematics was valid). The Eighth was not right (implementation violated the Third Mandate). The Eighth was something else—the category of knowledge that works but must not be used.

This would explain why the Bureau of the Lens exists. This is what they manage: kel-om-sera-veth, the true-and-forbidden.

*From notes dated Year 57:*

The Emperor's message tells me to bring "the cipher which was first, which the family has placed in the location where forgetting is stored." I do not know what cipher he means or where forgetting is stored.

Perhaps kel-om-sera-veth is not a description but a component of the instruction—a key or address I am meant to recognize when I find what I am looking for. Perhaps its meaning is irrelevant; only its function matters.

I have not found the cipher. I continue looking.

*From notes dated Year 69:*

I asked the Sri Pater about "the location where forgetting is stored." His response, four months later: the family has no record of such a place. No vault by that name. No archive. No ritual designation. Either the Emperor is referring to something the family has genuinely forgotten—which would be appropriate, if maddening—or he is referring to something that exists only in whatever space he inhabits.

I cannot search a space I cannot access. I cannot find a cipher I cannot identify. I continue looking because the Emperor told me to bring it, and if I am going to follow Kelso Senna through the Black Door, I would prefer to bring what was asked for.

Though I do not know why. Perhaps it matters. Perhaps nothing matters and I am filing requests for substances that do not exist and searching for ciphers that cannot be found and preventing deaths that would not have occurred.

This is my work. This is what I do.

## **Summary**

*From notes dated Year 64:*

I have been processing these missives for sixty-four years and have generated and discarded hundreds of interpretations. No conclusions.

The Emperor perceives something and reports it accurately. The reports are consistent across decades—the same garden, the same gardeners, the same receivers, the same vel-om, the same cycle of beauty and horror and forgetting. He is not confabulating or random; he is describing a stable reality that I cannot access.

Whether that reality is physical, computational, metaphorical, or something else, I cannot determine. The interpretations I can generate are constrained by my education, by what fragments survive, by my experience of temporal compression. None of them fit without remainder.

I have begun to wonder whether understanding was ever the point.

The Emperor's missive told me to bring the cipher when I follow Senna through the Black Door. Not *if* I follow—*when*. The Emperor knows I will go. Perhaps he knows what I will find there. Perhaps the revelation waits behind the door, and my sixty-four years of failed interpretation are merely preparation—a demonstration that I cannot understand from outside, that I must enter to see.

Or perhaps the Emperor knows nothing, predicts nothing, and the missive addressed to me is as confused as all the others. Perhaps Kelso Senna is another hapax—a term I will recognize without understanding, a face I will follow without knowing why.

I saw Senna in the archive corridor last week. He was younger than I have seen him before—barely adult, wearing robes that do not exist yet. He looked at me as if he knew me. I do not know if that was recognition or prophecy.

*From notes dated Year 71:*

I am tired.

I have been tired for decades, but the work provided structure, and structure kept the tiredness from mattering. Lately the structure feels less solid. I process the missives. I file what cannot be transmitted. I correspond with descendants I will never meet. I wait for my name to be called or for Kelso Senna to arrive and make all of this mean something.

That is the shape my hope has taken. I have stopped hoping the documents will help. I have stopped hoping pattern recognition will yield insight. Instead I hope that Kelso Senna—this stranger I have never spoken to, this ghost who appears in corridors wearing uniforms from futures that have not happened—will arrive one day in linear time and say: come with me. And I will go.

I have no evidence. The Emperor's instructions may be as confused as his judgments. But the alternative is processing missives until I die without ever learning what blooms on the south terrace.

I still hope the story will begin.

The kel-sera blooms. The receivers hunger. The Emperor reaches for whatever is closest and I prevent his reaching from arriving anywhere. Or I fail to prevent it and the prevention was always illusory. I do not know. I wait for the stranger who will make it make sense.

*From notes dated Year 78:*

The gun is still in my desk. I have not touched it in years.

Seven years ago I wrote about faith—about hoping that Kelso Senna would arrive and make everything make sense. I reread that entry this morning. The hope embarrasses me now. It was the hope of a younger man, which is strange to say about myself at sixty-four.

I still believe I will follow Senna through the Black Door. I have seen it. In the High Galleries of the First Whorl, I have passed myself—older, armed, bleeding, missing the fingers of my left hand. I did not stop. I was walking with purpose toward something I could not see from where I stood watching myself go.

But I no longer believe the door contains answers. I no longer believe the armed man I will become understands what I do not understand now. He was bleeding. He was maimed. He was following a stranger into the place from which no one returns. That is not the posture of someone who has finally grasped the truth. That is the posture of someone who has run out of alternatives.

I wonder now whether the Emperor's missive—"bring the cipher when you follow Senna"—was instruction or merely prediction. Whether he was telling me what to do or describing what he saw me doing. The Emperor perceives; the Emperor does not necessarily comprehend. He may have seen me following Senna through the door without understanding why, just as I have seen myself following Senna without understanding why.

Perhaps there is no revelation. Perhaps I will enter the Black Door and find only more confusion—a garden I cannot interpret, receivers I cannot identify, a vel-om whose nature remains obscure even when I am standing before it. Perhaps I will die there, having learned nothing, and my successor will inherit my notes and find them as useless as I found the fragments.

Or perhaps—and this is the thread of faith I cannot quite release—perhaps understanding requires entry. Perhaps the interpretations fail because I am interpreting from outside. Perhaps the armed man walking toward the door with purpose knows something I do not, will know something I do not, because he has crossed a threshold I have not yet crossed.

I keep the poems. Someone should know that the Emperor wrote them—that something inside whatever he has become remembers holding a hand when the stars were young and grieves for the loss. Someone should know there is still a person in there, even if I cannot reach him, even if my redactions protect populations he would destroy from the destruction he cannot stop commanding.

I mark the errors I cannot report on. I file what cannot be transmitted. I wait.

—T.

## **Translation Attempt**

*Working notes of Sri Lattice-Initiate Kalen Vor, Brotherhood of Proper Dominion, 4th Degree.*

### **On the Class VII Pronoun**

The Oracle speaks using the Class VII Indirect Reflexive—the same pronoun class as the Emperor-word. I render this as ONE-IN-FULLNESS, though this loses the intensive-plural aspect. There is no construction in any language I know for "that which is supreme and multiple and outside sequence, referring to itself indirectly because direct self-reference would imply an experiencing subject."

When the Oracle acts, it uses only atemporal verbs. Vel-ser: harmonize-into-unfolding. Vel-kel: harmonize-across-threshold (withhold from unfolding, keep beyond the boundary of disclosure). These are not actions in time. They are eternal states being made accessible or kept inaccessible.

### **Fragment 7.4**

KEN.OM.VAR-em \[VII.NOM\] sat.vel.keth-sah es-\[VII.GEN\]

SHEM.thal-em ya-OM.VAR tal.rem-kir shul.var.keth-om

*The origin-places-of-reception \[authority-class\] are delegation-judgment-voice-witnesses in-stead-of \[authority-class, genitive\].*

*The high-bloods at the place-of-reception \[are\] pattern-measured in four aspects at the obedience-reception-voice-surface.*

KENOMVAR-em takes Class VII marking. Reception-places are physical, temporal, countable—yet they take the atemporal-uncountable class. ### **Fragment 7.5**

\[VII.IND.REFL\] vel-ser: SHEM.thal-em kel-eth

# **Notes on Translation Difficulties in Pre-Interdict Technical Documentation**

## **Context**

The Companions Guild maintains the Cloister of the Testimony, which houses archives from the Technical Caste era. A Sri Companion seconded from the Ganati Synod was assigned to produce a maintenance handbook for feeding machinery based on pre-Interdict documents. These are his working notes on why the translation is impossible.

## **Working Notes**

*Sri Companion Sekh Tal-Omir, seconded to the Cloister of the Testimony from the Ganati Synod. Not for archive.*

### **Prefatory Remarks**

Eleven months with the feeding machinery documents. My task: produce a maintenance handbook that Guild technicians might follow. The originals are in late Technical notation—not the Jargon itself, but the pidgin that lower Technical Castes used with unmodified servants.

The difficulty is not technical content. Nutrient synthesis for Archon metabolisms can be described. The difficulty is grammatical. The pidgin encodes distinctions Imperial cannot express.

### **Two Categories of Vocabulary**

The first category comprises constructed terms: prefix-root-suffix assemblies recognizable from archaic Imperial. Velamethsīr appears to mean "evaluation-cycle-agent"—a component assessing metabolic states. The morphemes are opaque, but the assembly pattern is familiar. I can describe what the component does.

The second category is different.

Scattered through the documents are terms that do not decompose. They appear as irreducible units: sequences of vowels and voiced continuants with tonal contours marked through unfamiliar diacritics.

From my current working set:

* **āu-ī-zhem** (rising-falling-level): state transitions in biological substrates  
* **ō-vn-é** (level-rising): verification of nutrient composition  
* **ǔ-āzh-ō-ī** (falling-rising-level-rising): appears once, describing what happens when the feeding cycle fails

These are not Imperial. They are not constructed from morphemes. They are discretized approximations of Jargon coordinates.

### **The Continuity Problem**

The Jargon was sung, not spoken. Each utterance located a position in continuous semantic space. Nearby coordinates denoted related but distinct meanings. The distance between coordinates was itself meaningful.

You cannot write this down. Writing discretizes. The moment you transcribe a Jargon coordinate, you collapse a continuous position into a discrete symbol.

The terms I have listed are such collapses. The Technical Castes needed to communicate with baseline workers who could not sing. So they transcribed: this tone sequence, approximately here, close enough for immediate purposes. The approximation worked when living Technicals could clarify. Now we have only the points.

### **Translation Attempts**

The agglutinative terms translate badly. Velamethsīr becomes "the component which evaluates metabolic cycles." Meaning is lost, but the sentence authenticates.

The discretized approximations do not translate.

I have tried to render āu-ī-zhem:

"The state of transitioning between configurations" — rejected. The Lector flags the nominalized progressive as malformed when applied to non-temporal substrates.

"That which is becoming other than it was" — rejected. The construction implies temporal sequence. The contexts where āu-ī-zhem appears are explicitly atemporal.

"Transformation considered apart from sequence" — rejected. Imperial cannot form abstract nouns from processes without implying the processes occur in time.

The term occupies a semantic position Imperial grammar cannot reach. The position exists. The meaning is real—the documents use it consistently. But Imperial has no path to that location.

### **What This Implies**

The Jargon was a language of proofs. Every statement contained its own verification. False statements could not cohere.

If āu-ī-zhem derives from a Jargon coordinate, the concept it approximates is *proven*. Not believed. Proven, in whatever sense mathematical proof constitutes certainty. There genuinely is a form of transformation that does not occur in sequence. The concept is mathematical fact, collapsed into phonology that baseline humans could reproduce without being able to derive the proof.

And Imperial cannot say it.

### **On the Agglutinative System**

I am not a linguist. But eleven months with these documents has produced an impression.

The prefix-root-suffix structure of archaic Imperial was not arbitrary. It was an attempt to mirror something—to build linguistic architecture that could, through careful morpheme assembly, approach the continuous semantic space the Jargon described directly. The compositions were ladders. You could not reach Jargon meanings directly, but you could climb toward them through structured steps.

The understanding of what the morphemes were *for* was lost. The compositions remained, frozen into vocabulary. The Lectors enforced the freezing. Now the rungs exist but no one knows they were rungs.

### **The Political Observation I Should Not Make**

In Ganat we say: the cage that fits perfectly is the cage you cannot see.

The Empire has not changed in five Records. Its governance is frozen. Its technology is maintained but not developed. Its social structures reproduce themselves with mechanical fidelity. Scholars call this stability.

The discretized approximations—the Jargon-derived terms that name proven features of reality—are precisely the terms Imperial cannot translate. The concepts that are mathematically certain are the concepts the Empire's language cannot express.

I am not qualified to say what this implies. I note only that the coincidence is striking.

### **Practical Conclusions**

For agglutinative vocabulary, I provide Imperial approximations with notes on what the approximations lose.

For discretized approximations, I do not translate. I preserve the original notation and describe, as context permits, the semantic space each term occupies. Future technicians will not understand the terms. Neither do I. But they will know something is there—a proven concept the language cannot name.

*These notes may be copied among trusted colleagues. Do not submit to Guild archives. Do not file with any Bureau.*

*—S.T.O., third month of the Ascent, 631st year of the Record of the Black Star*

# **Memorial on the Propriety of Clarifications Regarding Jurisdictional Boundaries in Matters Touching Biological Administration**

**Submitted to the Archive of Procedural Precedent, Year 774 of the Present Ordering** **Sri Arbiter-Emeritus Kalen Morai, Bureau of the Scale**

---

## **I. On the Nature of the Question**

The matter before this body is not whether authority may be transferred between Bureaus. Authority cannot be transferred. Authority flows from the Emperor (SETAL) through the totality of delegation (es-est.ath) that obtains eternally; it does not move.

The matter before this body is whether certain exercises of function by the Bureau of the Creche, in periods prior to the present clarification, were exercises of delegated authority (sat.est) or assumptions of function without delegation.

If the former: then any restriction upon those exercises would require a construction of diminishment, which the Emperor's eternal delegation (SETAL.sat.ov.el) cannot grammatically undergo.

If the latter: then no restriction has occurred. What was never delegated cannot be withdrawn.

## **II. On the Scope of Creche Delegation**

The Bureau of the Creche holds authority over reproduction (varem.est)—authority over the tending of becoming, over the biological substrate upon which caste differentiation is maintained. This delegation is proper and uncontested.

The question is whether authority over reproduction (varem.est) includes jurisdiction to adjudicate reproduction (vel.est.varem).

Consider the construction. Jurisdiction (vel.est) requires adjudication (vel.ov) to occupy a subordinate position within a delegation chain. But adjudication (vel.ov) is not merely "decision"; it is reconciliation, the bringing of accounts into consonance. Adjudication is what the Bureau of the Scale performs. Jurisdiction (vel.est) is not delegated to the Creche; jurisdiction is delegated to the Scale.

The Creche's proper function is cultivation (varem.gir). The Creche may tend. The Creche may cultivate. The Creche may apply the techniques of biological maintenance to which its patents pertain.

The Creche may not exercise jurisdiction (vel.est). The Creche may not reconcile competing claims over whether a reproduction (varem.ov) shall occur. That is a Scale function. That has always been a Scale function.

## **III. On the Pre-Clarification State**

In periods prior to the present clarification, the Creche exercised termination, sterilization, and breeding-mandate without reference to Scale jurisdiction (vel.est). This exercise was not jurisdiction over reproduction (vel.est.varem). It was adjudication of reproduction without derivation from the Emperor (vel.ov.varem nem.es.SETAL): adjudication without derivation from the proper source.

Adjudication (vel.ov) without derivation from the Emperor (nem.es.SETAL) is systematic disorder (thar.eth). This is elementary. Adjudication that does not trace to the Emperor through a delegation chain is disordered. The grammar knows this; the Lector confirms it. One cannot authenticate adjudication as a source (vel.ov.ken) because authority must originate from the Emperor's eternal delegation (SETAL.sat.ov.el)—not from any act of adjudication by a subordinate body, including the Satara. The Charter does not and cannot originate authority; it can only disclose what was eternally delegated.

The Creche's pre-clarification exercises were, therefore, systematic disorder (thar.eth). Not intentionally. Not maliciously. But systematic disorder nonetheless—deviation from proper arrangement, disorder within the administrative fabric, function exercised outside proper chain.

## **IV. On What the Charter Accomplishes**

The instrument sometimes called the City-of-Glass Charter does not transfer authority. Transfer is not a concept the Emperor's eternal delegation (SETAL.sat.ov.el) admits. The instrument clarifies.

Clarification is disclosure. Disclosure is the Emperor's eternal unfolding (SETAL.ser.ov.el)—the unfolding of what obtains eternally but was previously unexpressed. When the Emperor discloses, no change occurs. What was true becomes visible.

The Charter discloses:

* That jurisdiction over reproduction (vel.est.varem) falls within Scale jurisdiction, as jurisdiction (vel.est) has always fallen within Scale jurisdiction.  
* That Creche function is properly cultivation (varem.gir), not jurisdiction over reproduction (vel.est.varem).  
* That exercises of jurisdiction (vel.est) by non-Scale bodies require delegated jurisdiction (sat.vel.est) from bodies holding that function—that is, require Scale approval.

None of this is new. None of this is change. The delegation chains obtaining at present are the delegation chains that obtained before the Malpais events. The Charter adds nothing. It removes nothing. It clarifies what the proper exercise of delegated function requires.

## **V. On Opposition to Clarification**

Those who oppose the present clarification argue, in effect, that the pre-clarification state was proper. They argue that adjudication of reproduction without derivation from the Emperor (vel.ov.varem nem.es.SETAL) was legitimate. They argue that the Creche's unilateral exercises were exercises of delegated authority (sat.est).

But this argument requires that jurisdiction (vel.est) was delegated to the Creche. It was not. Jurisdiction was delegated to the Scale. This delegation obtains in the structure of the Bureaus themselves, established by Throne's (SETAL.om.el) standing orders, which no Satara resolution can amend.

To argue that the Creche properly held jurisdiction over reproduction (vel.est.varem) is to argue that the standing orders delegated jurisdiction (vel.est) to both Scale and Creche. The standing orders do not so provide. The adjudicative function (vel.eth) is singular. It belongs to the Scale.

Opposition to the Charter is, therefore, opposition to the standing orders. Opposition to the standing orders is insurrection (ther.eth). Not systematic disorder (thar.eth), mere deviation—insurrection (ther.eth), revolt against the structure of delegation itself.

I do not accuse the Creche of insurrection (ther.eth). I observe only that their position, if accepted, would require a construction the grammar does not admit: that jurisdiction (vel.est) originates in multiple places, that jurisdiction can be delegated twice to different recipients, that the same adjudicative function (vel.eth) can belong to two chains simultaneously.

The Lector will not authenticate this. The Lector knows the chains are singular.

## **VI. On Procedure Going Forward**

The clarification establishes that jurisdiction over reproduction (vel.est.varem) requires Scale approval and admits Scale appeal. This is not new authority for the Scale. This is exercise of authority the Scale has always held but has not previously been called upon to exercise in this domain.

The Creche retains authority over reproduction (varem.est). The Creche may tend. The Creche may cultivate. The Creche may recommend. The Creche may petition the Scale for jurisdictional determinations (vel.est.varem).

What the Creche may not do is exercise jurisdiction (vel.est) unilaterally. What the Creche never could do—what no body without that delegation can do—is adjudicate (vel.ov) without reference to those who hold jurisdiction (vel.est).

The Charter changes nothing. The Charter restores proper procedure. The Charter clarifies what was always true.

I commend this memorial to the Archive, for such weight as precedent (neth.el) assigns.
