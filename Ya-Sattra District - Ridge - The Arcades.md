# The Arcades

> **Type:** Neighborhood (industrial/residential)
> **Location:** Tiers 450-572, interior of rotated arcology
> **Function:** ~90 archaeotech workshops, modest housing
> **Character:** Sideways spaces adapted across generations

The Arcades fill the arcology's internal spaces, where corridors and rooms designed for vertical habitation now lie sideways. Centuries of adaptation have transformed these spaces into workshops and modest housing.

## The Rotated Reality

What was a floor is now a wall. What was a ceiling might be the new floor, or another wall. Doorways cut through surfaces never meant to be penetrated. Structural columns that once ran vertically now jut horizontally through rooms.

**Elevator shafts** that ran vertically are now horizontal corridors. **Vertical movement** relies on rope lifts, pulley systems, and ladders rather than traditional stairs.

## Layered Adaptation

Each generation has added:
- New floors laid across old walls
- Doorways carved through what were ceilings
- Platforms creating usable surfaces where none existed

A room might have its "floor" built on what was originally a wall, with platforms constructed perhaps a hand's width above the original surface to create level walking.

The original architecture often becomes invisible beneath layers of adaptation.

## The Workshop District

**~90 workshops** recondition archaeotech from Rift salvage and Iron Yards operations. Work ranges from single-craftsperson shops to small businesses employing several specialists.

**Workshop conditions vary:**
- Some owners have invested in platforms, diagonal workbenches matching the slope, proper lighting
- Others work in spaces clearly still sideways—tools hung on what should be floors, materials stacked against geometry fighting them constantly

**The work demands real expertise:**
- Pre-Interdict designs employ principles current inhabitants only partially understand
- Repair requires reverse-engineering function from structure
- Improvising replacement components for parts that can't be replicated
- Modifying original designs to work with available resources

Ridge craftspeople develop deep knowledge of specific technologies—one might specialize in quantum lectors, another in mechanical computing devices, a third in arcane sensor arrays.

## Physical Experience

**Entering the Arcades:**
Corridors descend at uncomfortable angles. Walls show layers of modification where new construction obscures original architecture. The spaces feel both too narrow and too tall, proportions fundamentally wrong.

**Sound:**
Noise fills the air—hammering, grinding, hiss of compressed air, conversations shouted over machinery. Sounds don't echo properly, bouncing off modified surfaces in ways creating acoustic dead zones and unexpected amplifications.

**Navigation:**
- Requires understanding the sideways geometry
- Which corridors require handholds
- Where drainage pools if you're not careful
- How to move through spaces that aren't quite level

Children grow up navigating by feel, developing spatial skills that become unconscious by adulthood.

## Population

Junior workers, apprentices, and newer arrivals to the district occupy the Arcades. Interior spaces are modest and often awkwardly angled despite modification attempts.

**The trade-off:** Less comfortable than Horizon Row housing, but offering safety and proximity to employment the Middens cannot match.

Residents here are working to establish themselves—earning enough to live on the Ridge but not yet prosperous enough for Horizon Row or Penthouse Block.
