# Knife-Edge

> **Type:** Tenement / Industrial
> **Population:** ~1,600 residents
> **Function:** Metalworking, cable production, salvage processing
> **Notable feature:** Fifteen-degree structural lean

Knife-Edge leans fifteen degrees from vertical, a structural defect that would have condemned the building in any district with functioning inspection services. Instead, the lean has become the building's defining characteristic, shaping everything from furniture design to resident psychology.

## The Lean

**Current state:** Fifteen degrees from vertical, visible from anywhere in the district.

**Progression:** The lean is increasing—perhaps a degree every few decades—as foundations settle unevenly into saturated soil beneath.

**Prognosis:** Engineers estimate another century before genuinely uninhabitable. Current residents will be long dead before final collapse. Their children might live to see it. Their grandchildren almost certainly will. But that's someone else's problem.

## Adaptations to Angle

Every room shows adaptation to the fifteen-degree angle:
- Furniture sits on legs of different lengths
- Beds wedge against the high wall
- Liquids require covered containers or they slide toward the edge

**The tilted gait:** Children grow up navigating by feel, developing instinctive calculations of slope and grip becoming unconscious by adulthood. This gait marks Knife-Edge residents anywhere in ya-Sattra.

## Building Structure

### Ground Floor: Forge Operations

Coal-fired furnaces produce heat for metalworking:
- Cable production
- Tool repair
- Specialized components for salvage operations

The forges burn constantly, their smoke contributing to the haze over the eastern Middens.

**Communication:** Workers time conversations to gaps in the hammering—entire dialogues happening in spaces between strikes.

### Floors 2-4: Clan Venn Cable Works

Clan Venn maintains equipment for producing rigging that vertical life requires.

**Process:** Raw wire arrives from Iron Yards suppliers; finished cable departs for every building in the district.

The work requires precision despite the angled environment—tension must be consistent, splice joints must hold, failure means someone falls.

**Adapted techniques:** Workers have developed methods that wouldn't work in level spaces but produce reliable results in Knife-Edge's particular geometry. Eldin Venn can read metal fatigue by sound alone, hearing stress patterns predicting failure before visible signs appear.

### Upper Floors: Residential

Workers and their families occupy spaces above the industrial zone.

Every aspect of domestic life accounts for the angle:
- Children develop spatial awareness from infancy
- Technical intuition becomes unconscious by adulthood
- Level ground feels wrong to long-term residents
