# Clan Sarn: The Deep Dwellers

> **Location:** Minimal surface presence at Sump-Gate; real residence in flooded tunnels
> **Function:** Gatekeepers between surface sewer-fishers and deep populations
> **Trade:** Exotic deep salvage, technical knowledge
> **Mystery:** Nature of "deep populations" deliberately ambiguous

The Sarns trade with something in the deep that may no longer be human.

## Important Members

**Patriarch Vennas Sarn** (Age 71): Rarely surfaces, spending months in deep tunnels. Skin crusted with lichen; eyes have strange bioluminescent glow. Maintains relationships with deep clan leaders who only speak the Jargon. His mathematical intuitions about spatial relationships exceed even typical sewer-fisher capabilities—surface-dwellers have heard him call into darkness, listen for echo, then draw entirety of cavern complex.

**Navigator Liya Sarn** (Age 34): Primary deep scout and Vennas's daughter. Can navigate flooded tunnels blind, feeling depth in her inner ear. Lung capacity through training allows twelve-minute breath holds. Discovered three new deep populations in the last year.

**Korim Sarn** (Age 45): Surface representative and Vennas's nephew. Manages awkward position of translating deep clan needs into surface economic terms. Sometimes has difficulty explaining what deep clans are attempting to barter. Years above have made him "surface-soft" by clan standards, though he can still navigate depths lethal to others.

**Jerem Sarn** (Age 26): Korim's son, caught between worlds. Spent ages 10-18 in deep tunnels for traditional training but struggles with isolation. Serves as backup surface representative.

**Old Mett Sarn** (Age 83): Oldest sewer-fisher in ya-Sattra. Claims to have spoken with deep dwellers who remember the sterilization plague, tens of thousands of years ago. Stories might be dementia or genuine contact with populations predating surface catastrophe.

**Kenna Hasket-Sarn** (Age 31): Marriage bridge maintaining historical Hasket connection. Monthly trips coordinate upwellings from deep and Hasket aquaculture. One of few who can translate deep clans' mathematical concepts into surface applications. Has not found buyers for this knowledge.

**Toll Sarn** (Age 22): Shows disturbing aptitude for deep navigation—disturbing because he prefers it to surface life. Spends increasing time below. Has a romantic relationship with something in the deep which does not appear to be human. Vennas has asked. Answers grow more distressing each time.

**Vera Sarn** (Age 15): Being trained in clan's archive of deep-tunnel maps—less maps than probability clouds describing things which might exist Below. Ability to visualize three-dimensional flooded spaces exceeds even Liya's.

**Pen Sarn** (Age 39): Manages salvage processing. Deep salvage often requires decontamination or chemical treatment before surface trade. Workshop ventilated through natural chimney effects smells of industrial solvents and deep-tunnel minerals.

## Family Business and Relationships

The Sarns operate as gatekeepers between surface sewer-fishers and populations dwelling in tunnels flooded or abandoned since the Interdict. These deep populations—if Old Mett's stories are accurate—maintain infrastructure predating current Imperial systems. The Sarns trade surface goods (primarily chemical supplies and preserved food) for exotic salvage and technical knowledge.

Deep salvage differs qualitatively from surface finds. Pre-Interdict materials emerge in pristine condition, preserved by anaerobic environments. Technical components use alloys surface chemistry cannot replicate. Most mysteriously, occasional items show recent manufacture dates despite coming from zones supposedly abandoned for centuries.

The clan maintains three transition chambers at different depths. The first, twenty meters down, allows surface visitors with proper equipment. The second, at three hundred meters, requiring an eleven-minute dive, requires sewer-fisher physiology. The third, at unknown depth, only most experienced Sarns attempt. Each serves as trading post and decompression station.

The nature of deep populations remains deliberately ambiguous. The Sarns speak obliquely—"the below-ones" or "deep cousins." Whether these are deep sewer-fisher clans, other Technical Caste members, or something else entirely stays unclear. The Sarns themselves might not fully understand.

## Living Situation and Idiosyncrasies

The clan maintains minimal surface presence—reinforced basement beneath collapsed building at Sump-Gate. Surface quarters purely functional: sleeping spaces, Korim's negotiation room, Pen's workshop. Real clan infrastructure exists below, in partially flooded tunnels modified across generations.

Deep residence exists in managed flooding—spaces at specific water levels allowing both swimming and walking navigation. Furniture either weighted to stay submerged or suspended from ceilings. Important items in waterproof containers tethered at various depths.

Daily routine follows tidal influences affecting tunnel water levels—patterns differing from surface tides suggesting complex underground hydraulics. The clan rises and sleeps according to deep rhythms, making surface interactions temporally awkward.

Diet includes significant deep-tunnel resources. Bioluminescent fungi, carefully prepared to remove toxins. Blind fish from underground rivers. Most disturbing: certain lichens all other populations believe hallucinogenic. No apparent ill effects.

Communication with deep populations requires mathematical frameworks standard language cannot convey. The ancients called it the Jargon, but Sarns have reinvented it from first principles. Musical tones, pressure pulses, numerical sequences exchange information. Young members learn alongside spoken language, though some concepts resist translation entirely.

Physical adaptations manifest across generations. Younger Sarns show increased pressure tolerance, extended breath-holding, enhanced chemical resistance. Whether ongoing evolution or expression of latent Technical Caste modifications remains unknown.

Religious practices incorporate elements possibly from deep populations. They have NO Oracle shrine. Their shrine includes symbols not appearing in standard iconography—spirals that hurt to perceive directly, mathematical figures that seem to move despite being static. Prayers include prime number sequences feeling significant but lacking obvious meaning.

The question of deep-clan humanity haunts surface interactions. Korim carefully uses terms suggesting human populations below, but physical descriptions—when they slip out—hint at modifications exceeding even historical Technical Caste variations. The Sarns might be trading with populations that have diverged beyond recognition.
