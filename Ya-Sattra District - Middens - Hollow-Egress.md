# Hollow-Egress

> **Type:** Tenement / Five-clan cooperative
> **Population:** ~1,800 residents
> **Function:** Salvage access hub, clan governance center
> **Notable feature:** Lift-3 cargo elevator to pre-Interdict depths

Hollow-Egress represents the Middens' most successful experiment in cooperative governance. Nine stories of stable construction house approximately 1,800 residents under joint administration of five sewer-fisher clans who have created functional interdependence resisting both external exploitation and internal collapse.

## The Five Clans of Hollow-Egress

| Clan | Domain | Location in Building |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| **Loak** | Lift-3 operations, kitchen/intelligence | Ground floor operations, 8th floor kitchen |
| **Benru** | Documentation, legal existence | 5th floor archive |
| **Venn** | Rigging, cables, load distribution | Throughout building |
| **Hasket** | Security, aquaculture | Flooded basement |
| **Sarn** | Deep access, deep populations | Below second transition |

*Note: Clan Cho operates from Lantern-Heaps, not Hollow-Egress.*

## The Deep Infrastructure

The sub-level infrastructure extends far deeper than the building's visible height. Lift-3's shaft descends through pre-Interdict foundations into tunnel systems predating current understanding.

### Transition Chambers

**First Transition (20 meters):** Allows surface-dwellers with equipment. Regular salvage operations access this level.

**Second Transition (300 meters):** Requires sewer-fisher physiology—the ability to navigate spaces triggering claustrophobia in baseline humans, to breathe air carrying industrial contaminants, to perceive in darkness through senses Technical Caste inheritance provides.

**Below the Second Chamber:** The tunnels continue, but only Clan Sarn knows how far.

## Building Structure

### Ground Floor: Operations and Governance

The floor divides between Lift-3's massive doors and communal spaces enabling cooperative governance.

**Loading bays** handle daily flow of salvage ascending from below—exotic materials, pristine components, occasional items showing manufacture dates surface chronology cannot explain.

**Administrative section** houses representatives from each clan managing schedules, quotas, and revenue sharing that keep five families working together rather than against each other.

### Fifth Floor: Benru Archive

Documentation hub allowing sewer-fishers to work in a system that doesn't officially acknowledge their existence.

Every morning, workers arrive to collect temporary permits, work authorizations, and identity documents Korel Benru's staff produce from nothing but stamps and paper. The fiction is transparent—the same workers appear under different names in different ledgers—but the forms satisfy inspection requirements.

### Eighth Floor: Merra's Kitchen

The legendary operation where the perpetual stew has fed salvage crews for decades.

**The queue begins forming before dawn.** Positions determined by complex calculations: who found what below, who owes whom, which clans are cooperating and which are feuding this week.

**The kitchen is also intelligence.** Salvage crews discuss business while eating. Merra filters what she hears upward to Yuli. Information flows with the stew.

### Flooded Basement: Hasket Aquaculture

Clan Hasket maintains mussel beds in the building's flooded basement, producing protein supplementing the grain economy everyone else depends on.

Their unusual composition—big cousins fostered from stevedore orphans alongside little cousins of traditional sewer-fisher descent—creates capabilities neither population possesses alone.

## Lift-3 Operations

Clan Loak controls Lift-3 itself. **Yuli Loak** manages operations with the burn-scarred face testifying to her commitment—hot lubricant from a feedline explosion, an accident that would have killed someone less determined to maintain position.

The massive platform descends into darkness:
- Exotic materials from depths no one maps
- Pristine components from sealed pre-Interdict chambers
- Occasional items with manufacture dates that shouldn't exist

**What comes up** funds the Middens economy.
**What goes down** includes things the clans prefer not to discuss.
