# Entertainment in the Medina

> **Range:** Junior bureaucrat respectable recreation to street performers for thrown coins
> **Spectrum:** Upscale theaters to rougher pantomime stalls
> **Character:** Social lubricant enabling commerce and politics

## Bathhouses

**Respectable establishments:** Separate facilities for castes, hot/cold pools, steam rooms, massage services. Half obol basic access.

**Additional services (open secrets):** Private rooms rentable by hour. Attendants available for "personal attention" or "extended massage." Scale Bureau technically regulates; in practice, bathhouses pay fees functioning as licensing.

**Gradations are legible:**
- Certain symbols = particular preferences
- Highborn-derived staff = premium rates
- Transitional neighborhoods = fewer pretenses

**The spectrum:**
- **Aureate Baths:** Bathing without complications. Bureau clerks can visit without career risk.
- **Serpent and Spring:** Bathing is adequate; private rooms are the product.
- **Kelm's House:** Middle ground—options available but not presumed.

## Teahouses

**Standard offering:** Coca tea—dried leaves, mild stimulation, quarter obol per cup. Real product is the table where one sits for hours.

**Status symbol:** Westerner tea—ten times coca's price. Ordering it announces: can afford luxury, sophisticated taste, moves in circles where such things matter.

**Teahouse types:**
- Cramped stalls (standing customers, quick transactions)
- Elaborate establishments (private rooms, staff trained to facilitate discussions)
- Reputation spaces (where textile merchants gather, where reformist sympathizers speak freely)

The Lens monitors without suppressing—better to know where conversations happen.

## Bars

**Two products:**
- **Spiced beer:** Brewed locally from barley, flavored with herbs. Quarter obol per cup, moderate alcohol, consumed without ceremony.
- **Clear brandy:** Powerful, served in small cups for single swallows. Half obol per shot; signals serious engagement.

**Character by neighborhood:**
- Fourth Whorl: comfortable seating, decent food, named distilleries
- Transitional zones: rougher furniture, cheaper beer, uncertain brandy
- Archive Gate: honest drinks, utilitarian atmosphere

## Theaters (Licensed)

Several proper theaters presenting licensed productions under Lens oversight:
- Substantial buildings with stages, seating, lighting, scenic effects
- Admission 1-5 obols depending on seat quality
- Audiences: merchants, successful artisans, junior bureaucrats

**Political calibration:** Content attracts Lens condemnation without triggering suppression. **Elaborate condemnations function as endorsements.**

**Highborn vocalists:** Modified throats producing simultaneous harmonies. Drawn Aureate audiences willing to pay substantial admission. Arias composed for specific performers' unique capabilities.

## Pantomime (Rougher)

Informal performance spaces in converted shops, rented halls, street corners.

**Troupe size:** 3-6 performers
**Equipment:** Costumes, props, whatever staging space permits
**Admission:** Whatever audiences choose to give

**Stock characters:**
- The Pompous Bureaucrat quoting regulations while accomplishing nothing
- The Clever Sewer-Fisher solving problems through wit
- The Tsatsan Mystic Swindler claiming secret knowledge while picking pockets
- The Provincial Barbarian confused by civilization

**Advantage:** Can mock Bureau dysfunction in ways licensed theater cannot risk, then claim "mere entertainment" if inspectors inquire.

## Street Performers

Jugglers, acrobats, musicians working for thrown coins:
- Technically require permits no one possesses
- Survive by providing entertainment valuable enough that audiences pay and authorities tolerate

**Best performers:** Develop signature acts, incorporate comedy, work toward bar employment or theater orchestras.

## Polyphonic Singers

**Highborn bastards** whose ancestry includes modifications that never bred entirely true:
- Cannot match true Highborn vocalists' simultaneous harmonies
- Can produce overtones, undertones, resonances baseline singers cannot
- A single voice suggesting two or three voices

**Uncertain social position:**
- Not Highborn (lack full modification suite, Aureate registration)
- Not entirely baseline (capabilities mark them as something other)
- Perform on streets because no guild claims them, no theater will hire them

**Their music:** Haunting. Melodies acquire shadows, phrases resonate against themselves. Beautiful and unsettling.

**The Polyphonic Corner:** A plaza intersection where they've gathered to sing for generations. No one operates it. Singers come when they choose, coordinate informally.

**Administrative gap:** The Lens does not classify them as Highborn entertainment. The Creche does not register them as modified caste. They exist because categorizing them would require decisions no Bureau wants to make.
