# The Iron Yards

> **Type:** District (Rotten Quarter)
> **Population:** ~70,000 workers daily; ~45,000 residents
> **Function:** Salvage processing, industrial labor
> **Character:** Quarried depression between harbor and Ridge

The Iron Yards occupy a quarried depression in ya-Sattra's Rotten Quarter, carved from bedrock generations ago to build the arcology remnants that now define the city's skyline. The land itself records extraction—stone removed to construct what later fell.

## Geographic Foundation

**The gradient:** Northern edge drops toward harbor and the Rift; southern fringe climbs toward the Ridge.

**Zones:**
| Area | Location | Function |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Northern Docks** | Lowest level, harbor edge | Dry docks, hull work, massive salvage |
| **Central Zone** | Rising from docks | Salvage Dome, warehouses, cutting grid |
| **Torchline Row** | Elevated ridge | Commercial strip, services |
| **Southern Fringe** | Climbing toward Ridge | Worker housing (Forge Terrace, Breakspar Loft, Torchman's Rest) |

**Boundaries:**
- **West:** Blurs into Middens without clear demarcation
- **East:** Archive Gate—formal transition to Medina Quarter
- **South:** Climbs toward Ridge
- **North:** Harbor and Rift

## The Three Powers

| Organization | Controls | Leverage |
|--------------|----------|----------|
| **Salvage Guild** | Sales—every transaction passes through their auction | Charter monopoly, legal weight |
| **Pipefitters Union** | Labor—membership includes most workers | Willingness to strike |
| **Yen Tam Beneficial Society** | Vice—gambling, drugs, loans, enforcement | Debt cycles trapping workers |

**The Iron Yards Boys** (Hanged Men presence) extract percentages from salvage operations. The three major powers tolerate their extraction while working to prevent expansion.

## Physical Character

Walking through the Iron Yards means:
- Breathing metal and coal smoke
- Hearing constant percussion of industrial work
- Feeling heat radiating from forges and cutting operations
- Tasting hot iron and something chemical underneath
- Feeling ground vibrating with machinery that never stops

**The visual environment:** Salvage Dome rising above central district, warehouses spreading in patterns following underground infrastructure, catwalks and cable-lifts creating secondary street system above ground level.

## Economic Foundation

**Salvage flow:**
1. Material arrives from Rift (harbor), Middens (Lift-3), independent salvagers
2. Guild assesses everything, taking fees at every stage
3. Contractors (under master licenses) perform cutting and processing
4. Finished materials flow east through Archive Gate or ship out through harbor

**Debt cycles trap workers:**
1. Wages barely cover living expenses
2. Workers borrow from Yen Tams to cover shortfalls
3. Work extra shifts to service loans
4. Damage bodies faster, require more medical care
5. Need more borrowing
6. Cycle continues until broken → fall to Middens

## Worker Housing

**Forge Terrace** (~4,000 residents) — Transition zone; families live above/behind forges; industrial heat serves as winter warmth.

**Breakspar Loft** (~1,000 residents) — Shipping containers welded to cliff face; connected by catwalks; everything rusting.

**Torchman's Rest** (~4,500 residents) — Salvaged modular units stacked six high; creaks in wind but hasn't collapsed in three decades.

## The Archive Gate

Eastern boundary where industrial squalor meets Medina Quarter respectability. Everything passing through gets examined, documented, and taxed.

**Quarantine Shed** — Bureau of the Lens inspectors examine suspicious salvage. Inspector Drell Cassin: bribable within limits (overlooks paperwork, won't pass genuinely dangerous materials).

**Boundary economy:**
- Licensed porters carry materials for customers avoiding inspection
- Document preparers ensure paperwork meets Medina standards
- Broker Tess Mellish (8% fee) facilitates gray-market transactions

## The Iron Yards' Purpose

The Yards exist because salvage requires processing, and processing requires accepting costs respectable districts refuse:
- Medina won't tolerate forge smoke
- Ridge won't accept radiation-tagged materials
- Inner City pretends industrial economy doesn't exist

**The Yards take what other districts reject**, turning wreckage into wealth while destroying the workers who make the transformation possible.
