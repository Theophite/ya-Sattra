# The Gallows

> **Type:** Tenement / Organizational headquarters
> **Population:** ~200 Hanged Men plus support personnel
> **Function:** Hanged Men headquarters, water control center, tribute exchange
> **Notable feature:** Crane platform for public executions

The Gallows dominates the Middens both physically and psychologically. Fifteen stories of rust-stained metal and reinforced concrete rise from pre-Interdict foundations that have settled unevenly over centuries.

## Building Structure

### Lower Floors: The Exchange (1-3)

Ygren Fen's exchange market occupies the ground through third floors, where the Hanged Men's complicated economy resolves into fungible currency. Protection fees paid in salvage, food, labor vouchers, or more exotic currencies are evaluated by assessors with specialized knowledge of the district's informal markets.

The conversion rates favor the house, naturally, but maintain enough consistency that merchants prefer Fen-backed scrip to the chaos of direct barter.

### Middle Floors: The Barracks (4-8)

The organization's workforce occupies these floors. Fourth-floor residents share dormitory rooms and communal latrines; eighth-floor lieutenants have private spaces and windows that open.

This internal hierarchy creates competition the Knot encourages—men work harder knowing success means better quarters, that failure means descent to shared rooms. The barracks maintain military discipline despite the organization's evolution from militia to protection racket: morning formations, equipment inspections, perpetual readiness.

### Ninth Floor: Recreation and Surveillance

Gambling tables, drinking halls, and fighting rings provide release valves for violent men confined to barracks life. More subtly, the space allows Luka to observe his subordinates in unguarded moments—who drinks too much, who accumulates gambling debts, who shows dangerous ambition.

Sera Galen manages these spaces with attention to detail, understanding that entertainment is intelligence when properly observed.

### Leadership Floors (10-11)

Luka commands the entire north face of the tenth and eleventh floors; Jarek controls the south. An empty room reserved for Garren Fen (who physically cannot navigate the upper stairs) serves as reminder that power sometimes requires accommodation.

### Lockworks Command Center (12th Floor)

Riva Fen-Cho's command center contains maps accurately reflecting the district's hydraulic reality—every pipe, every valve, every point where water can be redirected, restricted, or shut off entirely.

### The Cisterns (13-14)

Six massive tanks hold months of water for the building plus reserves making the Hanged Men's distribution monopoly possible. Treatment facilities—sand filtration, chemical treatment, ultraviolet sterilization systems requiring Lantern-Heaps' electrical expertise—produce water that is reliably safe when nothing else in the district qualifies.

**The cisterns represent the organization's true power:** not violence, though violence matters, but control of something everyone needs to survive.

### Council Chamber and Crane Platform (15th Floor)

The Knot meets here monthly or when crisis demands. Seven seats arranged around a table salvaged from some pre-Interdict administrative facility, its surface scarred by decades of different purposes.

The crane platform extends from foundations running through the entire building's core, engineered to support loads the original structure never anticipated. The crane serves practical functions—lifting cargo from ships, moving materials between districts—but its symbolic purpose overshadows utility.

**Execution ritual:** The condemned is lifted at dawn, suspended over the district for one hour while residents observe, then dropped. The stories everyone knows create more control than the executions themselves.
