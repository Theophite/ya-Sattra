# Vetch-Rise

> **Type:** Tenement / Transit hub
> **Population:** ~2,500 residents (fluctuates weekly)
> **Function:** District entry point, transient housing, marginal commerce
> **Notable feature:** Sole pedestrian connection to mainland

Vetch-Rise occupies the liminal space between the Middens and the Iron Yards, serving as first landing for those desperate enough to accept any shelter and last resort for those with nowhere else to fall.

## Strategic Position

**Only building connecting directly to mainland.** Everyone entering or leaving the Middens on foot passes through here—or pays for boat transport.

This position explains why the Yen Tam Society maintains influence despite Hanged Men dominance elsewhere: they control access.

## The Absence of Structure

The building lacks institutional coherence:
- No dominant clan
- No industrial focus
- No unifying infrastructure

Population fluctuates weekly as arrivals and departures follow rhythms having nothing to do with the building and everything to do with circumstance.

## Yen Tam Society

The Yen Tam Beneficial Society provides theoretical protection from their third-floor offices, but their influence extends only as far as their ability to negotiate with Chain-Men who treat the building as hunting preserve.

**President Lao Duc** is identifiable by his missing fingers—tribute paid to maintain even minimal authority. He manages a treasury too thin to prevent all violence.

**The Society's protection:** Mainly preventing complete massacre, ensuring the building remains profitable enough to exploit rather than simply destroy.

## The Transient Market

The market assembles wherever Chain-Men aren't currently occupying. Vendors carry entire inventories ready to move at moment's notice.

**Goods available:**
- Used clothing marking wearer as employed rather than desperate
- Food that's technically still edible
- Documentation that might pass casual inspection

**When danger appears:** The market dissolves like smoke. Vendors vanish into stairwells and maintenance passages, reassembling elsewhere once danger passes.

This architectural guerrilla commerce requires no infrastructure beyond memory of where today's safe spaces might be.

## Building Structure

### Floors 4-5: Astal Warrens

The Astal population has claimed these floors, their tiny frames allowing subdivision of spaces larger bodies couldn't navigate:
- Platforms cutting rooms to half-height
- Bolt-holes drilled through floors
- Vertical warrens they prefer

**Unexpected stability:** Chain-Men find Astal spaces too cramped to exploit, while rapid Astal breeding produces population density deterring casual violence.

### Sixth Floor: Rain-Net Repair

The only genuine industry in the building. Nets from throughout the district arrive for patching:
- Polymer mesh capturing fog moisture
- Actual netting channeling rain
- Fabric sheets worn by constant weather exposure

The work requires delicate hands and patient attention, paying subsistence wages to residents who've found nothing better.

**Chain-Men rarely raid this floor**—disrupting rain-net repair would anger every building depending on water collection.
