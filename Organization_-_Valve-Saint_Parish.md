# The Valve-Saint Parish

> **Type:** Religious congregation (Penitent Church)
> **Location:** Overflow chamber, Sump-Gate (sea level)
> **Leadership:** Curate Aram es-Thabit
> **Function:** Spiritual community for industrial workers, grain distribution

The Valve-Saint Parish occupies the original overflow chamber in Sump-Gate—a vast space extending into the harbor where seawater seeps through the floor during high tide.

## The Worship Space

The floor sits below high tide, with grated sections allowing controlled water entry. Seawater seeps through during spring tides and storms, never more than ankle-deep but enough that congregants learn appropriate footwear.

**The altar** is the legendary wheel—four meters in diameter, corroded surface bearing generations of carved prayers.

### The Story of the Wheel

According to tradition: when catastrophic flooding threatened the building, a Redback stevedore whose name is lost turned this wheel that hadn't moved in centuries, activating the pumps through pure physical determination. His heart failed from the strain, but the water receded.

*The wheel probably never controlled the pumps—any engineer can see the lack of mechanical connection—but the story provides meaning that technical accuracy cannot replace.*

## Curate Aram es-Thabit

Aram conducts services acknowledging the building's industrial nature without surrendering to it:

- **Pumps as background rather than impediment** — the constant mechanical sound becomes liturgical rhythm
- **Tide as sacred timing** — services follow tidal schedules, not solar ones, accommodating workers following harbor rhythms
- **Mutterers above as witnesses** — their presence accepted rather than rejected

He rings disruption chimes hourly to prevent the cascading vocalizations that once resulted in three Mutterers attempting to rebuild a pressure valve while congregants were still arriving for service.

## The Congregation

The parish serves:
- Fishers whose lives follow harbor rhythms rather than sun cycles
- Pump workers dealing with machinery exceeding human understanding
- Anyone seeking meaning in mechanical systems

## The Grain Dole

The parish operates a grain dole from the adjacent kitchen, where **Deacon Mer Hasket** manages supplies.

**Requirement:** Attendance at services grants access to grain distribution.

This creates:
- Regular congregation regardless of spiritual motivation
- Dependency that the Hanged Men tolerate (religious authority is less threatening than secular alternatives)
- Documentation of who receives what and when

**The documentation habit:** Two years ago, Mer's obsessive record-keeping proved the parish wasn't selling donated food when an inspector arrived with accusations. The inspector left. The documentation habit remained.

## Political Position

The Valve-Saint Parish occupies a precarious position:

**Protected by Luka Fen** — not from sentiment but calculation. A martyred Curate would create more problems than a tolerated one. The parish provides social services the Hanged Men don't want to manage themselves.

**Tolerated by the Hanged Men** — religious authority channels discontent into manageable forms. Prayers are safer than protests.

**Essential to workers** — the parish provides meaning for people operating systems they don't understand, comfort for lives of industrial labor, and practical grain when other resources fail.
