# The Warborn

> **Status:** Combat-specialized caste
> **Origin:** Cloned in Hall of Mothers (ya-Sattra Second Whorl)
> **Lifespan:** Dead of cancer by 40 (universal, inevitable)
> **Variants:** Standard Infantry, Ironbone, Scout-Assassin

Warborn fill a room before they enter it. The sound of their breathing carries—deep, heavy, the respiration of bodies burning fuel at rates that would kill baseline humans.

## Universal Characteristics

**Cancer:** The cellular regeneration that lets them recover from wounds also produces malignancy. No Warborn reaches forty. The knowledge shapes everything about how they live.

**The Blink:** Autonomic violence response triggering under threat, bypassing conscious decision-making to initiate combat before higher cognition can intervene. Training channels this toward military applications; suppression is imperfect.

**Metabolism:**
- Six meals a day, each larger than baseline daily intake
- Three days without adequate food = combat-ineffective
- Body burns calories continuously, maintaining muscle mass and regeneration

**Heat:** They radiate heat constantly. Cooling systems in quarters are medical necessity, not luxury. Summer is miserable.

## Variant: Standard Infantry

**Type specimen:** Korath Venn, 26, Third Territorial Regiment

**Physical characteristics:**
- Nine feet tall
- Frame packed with muscle and reinforced bone
- Dark skin stretching over structures designed to absorb/deliver punishment
- Bald scalp showing nodular texture (bony protrusions from skeletal modifications)

**Origin:** Decanted in Hall of Mothers, raised in cohort with others of his variant.

**Daily life:** Duties primarily ceremonial—Empire rarely fights conventional wars. Trains daily anyway because metabolism demands it.

**Relationships:** Has a woman in ya-Don (textile worker). Cannot legally marry—Creche discourages Warborn reproduction. They maintain household together anyway. She understands she will outlive him by decades.

## Variant: Ironbone

**Type specimen:** Harath Kellum, 31 (ancient by Warborn standards)

**Physical characteristics:**
- Eight foot ten (shorter than standard, height traded for density)
- Calcium-reinforced skeleton with interlocking bony plates
- Armor BOLTS DIRECTLY TO BODY—steel studs emerging from bone
- Broader face, heavy jaw with calcium deposits

**Function:** Heavy weapons platform. Operates regiment's heavy railgun. Skeleton absorbs electromagnetic backwash that would shatter baseline bones.

**The cancer has begun:** He can feel it in his joints. Regimental physician estimates two years, perhaps three. He has not decided whether he wants to be lucky—slow decline seems worse than quick collapse.

**Letters:** He has begun writing letters to be delivered after his death. To the nurse who raised his cohort, to the seventeen survivors of his decanting. He does not want them to know how close the end is.

## Variant: Scout-Assassin

**Type specimen:** Senna Kol, 23, intelligence unit

**Physical characteristics:**
- Seven feet tall (short for her caste)
- Build optimized for rapid movement and enhanced perception
- Leaner, quicker than infantry cousins

**Sensory modifications:**
- Enhanced visual acuity (read documents at distances requiring magnification)
- Hearing sensitive enough to track conversations through walls
- Cost: bright lights cause pain, loud sounds trigger disorientation

**Heat signature:** Lower than standard Warborn. Harder to track with thermal detection. Operates longer between supply.

**Solitude:** Lives alone. Cannot tolerate sensory intimacy of another body in close proximity—heartbeat too loud, scent too complex, presence too much. Was not designed for companionship. Was designed for hunting in the dark.

**Death expectation:** Likely violent rather than cancerous—her work involves risks infantry never face. This seems preferable.

## The Chain-Men (Terminal Variant)

**Location:** Bright-Spire (Middens), Hanged Men enforcers

**Characteristics:**
- Terminal expression of Ironbone breeding
- Approaching 3 meters, bone plates covering face and body
- Will die of cancer by forty

**Notable:** Garren Fen XI (Knot member) represents this variant. Overlapping pale bone plates cover brow, cheeks, jaw, shoulders, forearms, knuckles. Face has abandoned human proportions.

**Chosen violence:** They could be anything, but they choose to be monsters. Pure enforcement without service provision rationale.
