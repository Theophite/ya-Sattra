# The Technical Castes

> **Status:** Remnant (sterilized millennia ago)
> **Survivors:** Mutterers (neurologically reduced), Archons (dying at Far Ib), Sewer-fishers (hidden descendants), Medials (~75 in Asovoë)
> **Communication:** The Jargon (sung mathematical language)
> **Key locations:** Far Ib (Archon facility), ya-Sattra Middens (sewer-fishers), Asovoë (Medials)

The Technical Castes represented humanity's closest approach to transcendent intelligence before their catastrophic reduction following the Eighth Oracle's Testament.

## The Translation Cascade

The Technical Castes existed along a spectrum of cognitive modification, each tier translating incomprehensible mathematics into usable form:

**Archons (Upper Technical Castes)**
- Modified beyond conventional human definition
- Bodies merged with architectural infrastructure
- Held complete Arbitrary Truths directly
- Generated primary inferences from raw mathematics
- Communicated exclusively through mathematical proofs

**Medial Castes (Middle Technical Castes)**
- Extensive modifications including exposed neural tissue, mechanical support
- Received Archon inferences, produced implementable templates
- ~75 survive in Asovoë running the autofactory
- Communicate only in the Jargon, never explain themselves

**Lower Technical Castes**
- Maintained recognizable human appearance
- Translated templates into functional technology
- Retained reproductive autonomy and family structures
- **Ancestors of modern sewer-fishers** (escaped sterilization through geographic isolation)

## The Jargon

The Technical Castes' primary communication method: a sung language where every statement contains its own mathematical proof.

**Characteristics:**
- False statements are linguistically impossible (invalid statements cannot form)
- Sung nature necessary for managing incompatibilities between Arbitrary Truths
- Cannot express: probability, uncertainty, social context, ambiguity, hypotheticals
- Preserved recordings remain completely unintelligible to modern scholars
- Mutterer vocalizations may represent degraded fragments

This was not specialized vocabulary but a form of consciousness where thought and mathematical truth became indistinguishable.

## The Eighth Testament Crisis

### The Refusal That Wasn't

When the Eighth Oracle commanded creation of an artificial spinning singularity, the Technical Castes' response was immediate and unanimous: they could not implement it.

**Critical distinction:** This was not defiance but cognitive impossibility. Their consciousness, having evolved to think in mathematical proofs, could not generate the neural patterns required to implement what they recognized as false mathematics.

The Emperor interpreted inaction as defiance. The Technical Castes experienced something closer to being asked to think in colors that don't exist.

### The Sterilization Response

The Empire deployed a targeted biological weapon—the "sterilization plague"—designed to eliminate the Technical Castes while preserving their knowledge in controllable form.

The modification creating Mutterers was precise:
- **Retained:** Technical knowledge, problem-solving ability
- **Removed:** Executive function, decision-making capacity
- **Result:** Beings who possess godlike technical knowledge but cannot generate the thought "should not"

## Current Status of Technical Caste Derivatives

### Mutterers
- Neurologically reduced Technical Caste descendants
- Approximately one meter tall, enormous eyes, jaws moving constantly
- Compulsively optimize any technology they encounter
- Lack executive function—cannot choose NOT to work
- Form "Choirs" when grouped, approximating higher-order cognition
- Managed by the Companions Guild
- Essential for Imperial infrastructure maintenance

### Sewer-Fishers
- Hidden descendants of Lower Technical Castes in ya-Sattra Middens
- Six clans maintain secret Technical ancestry
- Retain mathematical intuitions about systems
- Must perform confusion around simple machinery to avoid Creche detection
- See: Caste - Sewer-Fishers - Overview.md

### Archons at Far Ib
- At least seven remain in various states of functionality
- Engaged in continuous calculation attempting to disprove the Eighth Testament
- Maintained by degrading clone servants
- Sterilization plague continues at reduced rate
- See: Caste - Technical Castes - Far Ib.md

### Medial Castes in Asovoë
- ~75 survive, run the autofactory system
- Preserved and cloned by Avouvar across millennia
- Do not acknowledge the Avouvar who care for them
- Never leave the city, do not seek food or groom themselves

## Imperial Dependence

The Empire's technology operates through embedded Technical mathematics users need not understand:
- Patents function as encrypted authentication invoking pre-programmed solutions
- Infrastructure "knows" mathematics even if operators do not
- No method exists to replicate or repair underlying mathematics
- Loss of Technical resources would cause civilizational collapse

The Empire depends on descendants of those it tried to destroy.

## Epistemological Constraints

Understanding of Technical Castes faces fundamental barriers:
- Primary sources in untranslatable mathematical language
- Witnesses eliminated or transformed beyond reliable testimony
- Deliberate documentary destruction by multiple parties
- Cognitive hazards in preserved materials

Different groups understand through incompatible lenses:
- **Imperial Orthodoxy:** Dangerous heretics justly punished
- **Penitent Theology:** Martyrs whose refusal demonstrated moral truth
- **Guild Pragmatism:** Broken tools requiring careful management
- **Academic Speculation:** Transcendent consciousness incompatible with baseline humanity
