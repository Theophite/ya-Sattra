# Lantern-Heaps

> **Type:** Tenement / Electronics salvage center
> **Population:** ~2,200 residents
> **Function:** Electronics recycling, electrical distribution, Ridge connection
> **Notable feature:** The only well-lit building in the Middens

Lantern-Heaps exists because ya-Sattra produces more electronic waste than its formal recycling systems can process, and someone realized that waste contained value. Eight stories of salvaged shipping containers, industrial modules, and improvised construction house approximately 2,200 residents, most employed in sorting, testing, and resale of electronic components.

## The Cho Monopoly

The Cho family controls the operation through ownership of the transformer station providing the building's power.

Their hybrid heritage—sewer-fisher technical intuition combined with Fen organizational instincts from a marriage three generations past—created expertise to maintain electrical systems that formal engineering couldn't service.

**Rainbow-colored lights** mark Cho territory throughout the Middens, advertising their monopoly: if you want electricity, you deal with the Chos.

The family's technical expertise extends beyond distribution—they maintain the Lockworks' control systems under contract to the Knot, their knowledge of water infrastructure making them essential despite their hybrid status.

## Building Structure

### Ground Floor: The Chip Bazaar

Tables arranged with obsessive precision display sorted electronic components:
- Capacitors clustered by voltage rating
- Resistors by resistance value
- Integrated circuits by package type and suspected function

**Child labor:** Children as young as six work desoldering stations, their small fingers nimble enough for precision work that adult hands struggle with.

**Specialized jargon:**
- "Pregnant capacitor" = bulging from heat damage
- "Whisker bridge" = tin dendrites shorting adjacent pins
- "Magic smoke escaped" = the component is dead

### Sorting Floors (1-3): The Value Gradient

**First floor:** Bulk scrap—wire by the kilogram, boards stripped of obvious components, materials destined for metal recovery rather than reuse.

**Second floor:** Testable components, each piece checked against salvaged specification sheets before pricing.

**Third floor:** Premium items—working processors, intact memory modules, the rare functional assemblies emerging from salvage stream intact.

### Upper Floors: Cho Apartments

Family spaces distinguished by reliable lighting that elsewhere in the Middens requires careful rationing.

**The contrast is deliberate:** Visitors see what consistent electricity provides, understand what they're paying for when they negotiate Cho rates.

### Eighth Floor: The Gondola Station

A reinforced platform with cables stretching to the Ridge where Sera Galen's ancestors established the connection decades ago.

**Cost:** Four obols round trip—expensive enough to limit casual traffic, cheap enough that workers can afford it when necessity demands.

**Significance:** The only direct Middens-Ridge link, carefully controlled by a single family profiting from monopoly while maintaining just enough accessibility to prevent alternatives from developing.
