# The Ridge

> **Type:** District (Rotten Quarter)
> **Population:** ~1,200
> **Function:** Archaeotech reconditioning, infrastructure control
> **Structure:** Fallen arcology (Ninth Horizon Terrace) now horizontal

The Ridge occupies the uppermost tiers (450-600) of the Terrace of the Ninth Horizon, a pre-Interdict arcology that collapsed sideways in antiquity. The massive structure—nearly a mile tall when it stood vertical—now lies at an angle, creating a ridge almost 100 meters high.

## Geographic Position

The Ridge forms the southern boundary of the Rotten Quarter:
- **North:** Elevated above the Middens
- **Northwest:** Above the Iron Yards
- **East:** Rubble road connects to Medina Quarter at roughly equal elevation

The arcology didn't land flat—it settled at a west-southwest tilt on ground sloping toward the bay, creating spaces fundamentally sideways relative to actual gravity.

## The Three Zones

| Zone | Tiers | Character |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **Horizon Row** | 450-572 (exterior) | Commercial street on top of rotated wall; almost feels normal |
| **Penthouse Block** | 573-600 | Elite residences; separated by tidal canal, accessed via rope bridge |
| **The Arcades** | 450-572 (interior) | ~90 workshops in rotated interior spaces |

## The Three Families

The Ridge's stability depends on three infrastructure families controlling essential services through complementary monopolies:

| Family | Controls | Key Asset |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Val** | Water | Deep standpipes to ancient reservoirs |
| **Marzen** | Electricity | 3 MW thorium micro-reactor |
| **Galen** | Information | Grey Sheet intelligence, encryption services |

**Why they can't destroy each other:**
- Vals need Marzen electricity to run their pumps
- Marzens need Val water to cool their reactor
- Galens need both for printing operations
- All benefit from Galen intelligence preventing surprises

## Adapted Living

Living in a sideways arcology creates specific challenges:
- What was a floor is now a wall
- What was a ceiling might be the new floor
- Doorways cut through surfaces never meant to be penetrated
- Structural columns that once ran vertically now jut horizontally

**The tilted gait:** Ridge residents develop characteristic movement from navigating angled spaces. Even on level ground elsewhere in ya-Sattra, they stand as if compensating for a lean that isn't there.

## Self-Governance

The Ridge lacks formal Imperial administration:
- No Bureau maintains permanent offices
- No appointed officials collect taxes directly
- No Imperial courts handle disputes

Instead, the three families make significant decisions through consultation. The Ridge Night Watch—community-funded patrol staffed by mustered-out levy soldiers—provides security.

**Cardinal rule:** Illicit or disruptive activities must remain out of public view. The Watch enforces the distinction between private business and public disruption.

**Qirren Val** serves as the only formal Imperial presence—Bureau of the Lens inspector for the Rotten Quarter. His family connections mean he understands district realities rather than just official fictions.

## Economic Foundation

The Ridge centers on archaeotech reconditioning—restoring, repairing, and modifying pre-Interdict technology from Rift salvage and Iron Yards operations.

**~90 workshops** in the Arcades produce reliable results at reasonable prices. Medina Quarter merchants and even some Aureates bring archaeotech here because Ridge craftspeople deliver quality.

This expertise justifies the Ridge's middle-class status. Craftspeople insist on being treated as professional equals, and the quality of their work makes that insistence credible.
